# partsmith-features

`Feature` trait, `Registry`, `BuildContext`, `FeatureOutput`, and the built-in features (sketch, extrude, revolve, fillet, chamfer, shell, hole, primitives, booleans, patterns, mirror, datums, and more).

Spec (the definition of done): `docs/spec/features.md` and `docs/spec/features/<type>.md`. Acceptance tests in `tests/` come from features.md sections 6.2 and 6.3; document tests of section 6.4 and of every feature spec live in `crates/partsmith-recompute/tests/`. Do not weaken them.

## Public interface

The modules of features.md section 2.1 are public: `feature`, `deps`, `params`, `context`, `output`, `error` (errors, warnings and message parts), `registry` (with `register_builtins`), `python` and `builtin` (one module per type). The crate root re-exports everything below.

- Constants: `VERSION`, `FEATURE_API_VERSION`, `OCCT_VERSION` (re-exported). Manifest types re-exported from `partsmith_model::manifest`: `FeatureManifest`, `Category`, `FieldHint`, `FieldUnit`, `SelectionKind`, `OptionHint`.
- Contract: `Feature` (with provided `validate` and `python`), `DynFeature`, `Dependencies` (`Link`, `AssetLink`, `ExprUse`; helpers `add_feature`, `add_body_read`, `add_body_written`, `add_expression`, `add_asset`, `add_plane`, `add_axis` keep the no-duplicate rules).
- Shared params: `BodyOp`, `CopyOp` (`is_default`), `ProfileRef`, `PlaneRef` (`Default` is origin XY, `is_default`), `AxisRef` (an edge or face axis keeps the picked `direction`, a unit vector checked on deserialization and omitted when absent; `ctx.axis` does not read it yet, section 3.6's frames come with phase 2), `OriginAxis`, `PlacementExt` on `partsmith_sketch::Placement` (`offset`, `flipped`, `local_to_world`, `to_kernel`), and `point` and `vector` (kernel `Point` and `Vector` of `[f64; 3]`). Primitives share `PrimitivePlacement` (`add_dependencies`, `frame`, `python_keywords`, `hints`) with `default_position` and `is_default_position` (section 3.14), and `add_position_default`, the `#[schemars(transform = ...)]` that writes `["0", "0", "0"]` as the schema default of `position` and of move body's `translation`, which schemars leaves out because `skip_serializing_if` skips it (section 3.3).
- Context: `BuildContext::new(BuildInputs)` (with `history`, each declared body's writer chain) with `feature`, `units`, `field` (a `FieldRef` labeled from the manifest), `eval_length/angle/scalar`, `eval_count(field, expr, minimum)` (checks the minimum, then the u32 range, then the whole number, so every suggested fix works; patterns pass 1), `body`, `body_history`, `sketch`, `datum`, `asset`, `profile`, `resolve` and `resolve_all` (section 3.8: follows are `ReferenceFollowed` notices, guesses and widenings warnings, every loss a `ReferenceLost` or kept in `ReferenceNotFound` and `ReferencesNotFound`), `resolve_all_usable` (with a `Usable` predicate, so a field never offers a candidate it cannot use; fillet and chamfer drop smooth edges), `plane` and `axis` (each a `WithWarnings` value with the reference's warnings and notices; planes drop curved candidates, axes curved edges and faces without an axis), `face_placement` (the face placement rule with a caller's hint; the inner `Err` is a `FallbackReason` for an empty body, a missing or ambiguous reference or a face that is not flat; until phase 2 brings `FacePlacement::warnings` (section 3.6, features/sketch.md test 32) it uses a guessed face without a `reference_guessed` warning, which cannot move the sketch, since every piece of a flat face lies in its plane), `is_canceled`. `Values::evaluate` and `iter`; `BodyState` (`empty`, `new`, `from_update`, `shape`, `naming`, `is_empty`, and `topology`, `elements`, `element_set` (the resolver's view of the state, `ElementSet::new` of `elements` with the map's aliases and successions) and `volume`, each cached and shared by clones, the first and last carried from the update's `Measurements` when the feature measured them and the carried topology has the shape's element counts; `elements_computed` tells whether `elements` was computed; `name_set` is the names-only view (`NameSet::new` of the naming map, cached and shared by clones, never computing `elements`), which recompute's loss diagnosis walks writer chains through; `motion` is the naming map's motion of a face name, the identity for an empty body; `measurements` returns what is cached, for an update that keeps the state; `apply_op` and hole read the body's volume through it); `Resolved` (elements, sources, warnings, notices); `WithWarnings`; `ResolvedProfile` (regions, the successions of boundary matches, warnings and `RegionSplit` notices; `ProfileNotFound` keeps a `RegionLost` per region) with `faces()` (the union of the resolved regions as kernel faces, `ProfileFaces { profile, entities }` where curve tag `i` is `entities[i]`); `CancelToken`.
- Output: `FeatureOutput` (with `absent`: `AbsentFace` and `Absence`, the faces a cut extrude or hole leaves `Through` or `EndOutside` and the caps of a full revolve, `FullTurn`), `OutputData`, `BodyUpdate` (with `measured`), `BodyChange`, `Measurements` (the topology and volume a feature measured on its result, features.md section 2.7), `SketchResult` (`motion`, identity until frames come with phase 2; `point`, `to_world`), `DatumResult`, `Tool`, `VOLUME_TOLERANCE` (1e-9, the relative volume of the body rules), `apply_op` (the body semantics of section 3.5 with the boolean naming rule) and `apply_op_result` (the same rules applied to a kernel boolean the feature made itself; `Internal` unless the history has one input per body and tool with that shape's element counts). `apply_op` passes every tool to one kernel `fuse`, `cut` or `common` call (intersect keeps what lies inside every tool), then calls `apply_op_result`; it fuses several tools for op new and returns `Internal` without tools. Join, cut and intersect updates carry the result's topology and volume, a `NoEffect` update the body's own `measurements`.
- Naming bridge: `topology_info` (kernel topology and fingerprints for `partsmith_naming`), `name_shape` (with the successions and the new faces' motion, section 3.9) and `name_by_history` (the boolean rule with the `<feature>/face` fallback and its motion), which return `Named` (the naming map and the `topology_info` it was built from, for `Measurements::topology`), and `modified_sources`. A name too long to build is `NameTooDeep`.
- Messages: `FeatureError` (with `internal(message)` and `From<KernelError>`, which gives `Kernel { error }` so `?` works on kernel calls) and `FeatureWarning`, each with `render(&RenderContext)`, `fixes(&RenderContext)`, `code`, `field`, `severity`, `suggestion`, `detail` and `repairs`/`repair`; `Display` renders with ids, millimeters, `this feature` and no diagnosis. `RenderContext` (feature, `Names`, units, the bodies before the feature, `Diagnosis`), `Diagnosis` (`Explanation` per loss, `root`, `Because`, `by`), `Loss` (with `matches`, the count of elements that fit an ambiguous reference, which messages give as `{n}`), `LossCandidate`, `RegionLost`, `Fix`, `Place`, `Severity`, `Repair` (`Element` with its loss, `Region`), `describe_elements` (section 3.12, elements of a body in its state just before a feature, `before`, or at the end of the timeline; messages pass their own feature) and, for recompute's reason clauses, `describe_face` (a face of a body by its name alone, read by the face of that name in the state, with the same `before`), `describe_copy` (`third copy`, `copy in row 2, column 3`, `mirror image`), `made_from_line` and `sketch_coordinates`, `FieldRef` (`new`, `pointer`), `Bound`, `Noun`, `IgnoredReason`, `FallbackReason`, `Names` (body names, feature labels, type labels, records, sketch results and, provided empty, the faces an output records absent, the records in timeline order, `built` (the feature has an output in this run) and `value`, a record's expression evaluated with the document's parameters), `DocumentNames` (with the run's outputs), `format_value`. Phase 3 rows of section 4 (limits, `NoEffectHow`, a target for `BodyNotEmpty`) keep their earlier texts. `too_large_search` is the bisection of section 4.3.
- Registry: `Registry` (`new`, `with_builtins`, `register`, `register_dyn`, `get`, `types`, `validate_and_canonicalize`, `params_schemas`, `dependencies`, `build`, `python`, `python_generic`, `label`, and `FeatureInspector`), `Canonical`, `Validated`, `RegistryError`, `register_builtins`.
- Python: `PythonCall` (`new`, `render`), `PythonValue` (`from_json`, `expression`, `length`, `angle`, `reference` (with `anchors=` and `merged=` when stored), `region` (with `outline=`), `plane`, `axis` (with `direction=` when stored), `render`), `PythonNames`.

## Internals

- `deserializer.rs` reads typed params straight from the JSON map with its own serde error type, so `InvalidParams` details name the deepest field path without parsing serde messages. Unknown root keys are the input keys the params schema does not know: `schema::known_keys` collects the root `properties` and those of every root `$ref`, `anyOf`, `oneOf` and `allOf` branch, so the fields of a flattened enum are known keys, reserved-name checked and need hints.
- `schema.rs` holds the registration checks (open object root, reserved keys, `$defs` conflicts, manifest against schema through `$ref`, `anyOf`, `oneOf`, `allOf`, `items` and `prefixItems`).
- `profile.rs` builds the region union by dropping boundary pieces that two selected regions share (matched exactly, both directions) and chaining the rest through the arrangement's own loop successors; components (joined by shared pieces) give one outer loop each. `merge_pieces` then joins consecutive pieces of a loop (the last and the first included) that come from one entity, run the same way and meet exactly, into one line or arc, on every loop (a single region's too); a loop that is one entity all the way round becomes its circle, and otherwise a run never grows back to its own start (`closes`), so a boundary that touches itself on a circle keeps that circle's arcs apart. Pieces of an entity with another piece between them stay separate (naming numbers them `#n`).
- `context/` holds `BuildContext`, its reference lookups and `CancelToken` (`mod.rs`), `BodyState` with its cached measurements (`body_state.rs`) and `Values` (`values.rs`); `mod.rs` re-exports the last two, so `crate::context::BodyState` and the root exports are unchanged.
- `context/references.rs` resolves stored references by meaning (section 3.8: follows, guesses, widenings, losses, the candidates a field can use and the "one carrier" test); `context/regions.rs` resolves profile regions (section 3.7).
- `error/` holds `FeatureError` (`mod.rs`), `FeatureWarning` (`warning.rs`), `FieldRef` and field paths (`field.rs`), fixes (`fix.rs`), losses and repairs (`loss.rs`), the render model (`render.rs`: `RenderContext`, `Diagnosis` and the crate's `Renderer` with the shared reason and fix helpers of section 4.4), element descriptions (`describe/`: `mod.rs` the rules for edges and vertices (a vertex on one face reads by its position), `face.rs` face facts and words (a cap a cut records absent with `EndOutside` makes its element read as the tool's own, corpus R30 (a), and so does a tool face of a cut boolean of which the state has no piece, R30 (b)), `caps.rs` the direction a cap of an extrude or revolve faces in this run when it is not in the state (a revolve's from the axis in its record, the side of it the first region's stored centroid lies on and, for an end cap after a partial turn, `Names::value` of its angle; none for the end cap of a full turn), `cuts.rs` the cut boolean whose tool faces a feature's faces are in the body described (the faces followed through the later records that built in this run, up to the feature the state is just before: booleans, keeping their tools or not, and mirrors, patterns and splits into a target), `maker.rs` the feature that made a face, `name.rs` names taken apart), and the message parts (`message.rs`: names, `format_value`, and the suggested-limit text, which cuts two significant digits from the shortest decimal form so it never exceeds the value).
- `test_support.rs` (tests only) has a `Fixture` that owns a context's inputs, `values` (evaluated uses), `reference` (a stored reference from its JSON), named box and primitive tools, `ScriptNames` for Python hooks, and helpers that build a feature from JSON params over a `Fixture` without a timeline (`params`, `params_with`, `fixture`, `build`, `updates`, `update`, `updated_shape`, `assert_volume`, `assert_bounds`, `python`, `python_with`). Unit tests cover what a feature computes on its own; whole documents of built-in features are tested in `partsmith-recompute`.
- `tests/common/mod.rs` has the `Probe` and `Slab` test features, `ContextFixture` (with the writer chains of `BuildInputs::history`), `built_body`, which builds a solid record of the built-in registry (a box or a cylinder of the feature specs) into a body state, `NoNames` and `LabelNames` (fixed labels for hand-built messages) and render helpers that make a `RenderContext`. `tests/common/timeline.rs` builds built-in records in timeline order the way recompute does (states, writer chains, outputs, no cache or diagnosis), so build context and message tests read real body states (`run_with_parameters` adds document parameters, which builds and `Names::value` read), render with the document's names and pass the `Diagnosis` a scenario implies (`with_names` gives those names with the run's outputs, `with_end_context` the render context of a suppressed feature over the end states, for references resolved through `fixture`); `tests/common/records.rs` writes the specs' shared setups (`BLOCK`, `SLOT(a, b)`, `CUBE10`, `CUBE20`, `BOX-BELOW`); `tests/common/lint.rs` is the message lint of the error guidelines. `tests/descriptions.rs` checks one rule of section 3.12 per test beyond section 6.5 test 1 (with `describe_before` for a state just before a feature), and `tests/followed_messages.rs` the exact texts of `reference_followed` and `reference_widened` for each follow, with and without `Diagnosis::by`.
- `tests/budgets.rs` times the framework budgets of features.md section 5 with `std::time::Instant` in release builds (all seven, the `BodyState::elements` budget of a 200-face body and the `describe_elements` budget of five pocket edges after 100 records included, are skipped in debug builds).

## Built-in features

Each type lives in `src/builtin/<type>.rs` (`hole` in `src/builtin/hole/`) and is registered by adding `Registry::register::<Type>` to `BUILTINS` in `src/builtin/mod.rs`. All 20 types of features.md section 3.13 are registered, so the acceptance tests that need them run by default.

Registered types:

- Primitives (features.md section 3.14): `box` (`BoxFeature`, since `Box` is the standard library's pointer), `cylinder` (`Cylinder`), `sphere` (`Sphere`), `cone` (`Cone`), `torus` (`Torus`). Each params struct puts its sizes first, then the shared `plane` and `position` (`PrimitivePlacement`), then `op`; sizes are checked in field order before any kernel work by the positive length rule of features.md section 3.4 (`PrimitiveParams::positive_sizes`, through `common::positive_length`: below `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, 1e-6 mm, a size is `OutOfRange` (`GreaterThan(0)`) like 0; a torus also needs its ring at least that much wider than its tube), faces are named `<feature>/<role>` from the kernel's `PrimitiveFace` roles (`common::primitive_tool`), and the tool goes through `apply_op`. The box shifts the kernel's corner-anchored frame by half its length and width; a cone treats a radius below `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` (1e-6 mm) as an apex and builds a kernel cylinder when its radii differ by less than that.
- Sketch-based features (features/sketch.md, datum_plane.md, extrude.md, revolve.md, hole.md): `sketch` (`SketchFeature`, params `partsmith_sketch::SketchParams`, no Python hook because the engine writes sketch blocks), `datum_plane` (`DatumPlane`), `extrude` (`Extrude`, with `ExtrudeExtent`), `revolve` (`Revolve`, with `RevolveExtent`) and `hole` (`Hole`, with `HoleType` and `HoleExtent`). A sketch on a face is placed by `BuildContext::face_placement` with its stored fallback as the hint, and keeps that fallback with a `PlaneFallback` warning when the body is empty or the face is missing, ambiguous or not flat; it evaluates only driving dimensions and maps the solver's result to `SketchInvalid`, `SketchConflict`, `SketchNotSolved` or a `RedundantConstraints` warning. Extrude and revolve sweep `ResolvedProfile::faces()` through `common::swept_tool`, which names the tool with `sweep_tool` (the profile's successions as lateral successions, the sketch's motion), then apply the op after the profile's warnings and notices (`common::profile_messages`); a cut extrude records an end cap its result lacks as absent (`Through` without a start cap in the result, else `EndOutside`), and a full revolve both caps (`FullTurn`); through all goes 1 mm past the farthest bounding box corner along the direction (`common::farthest_corner` and `common::THROUGH_ALL_MARGIN`, shared with hole, decision D21). Every length they check uses the positive length rule (`common::positive_length` and `is_positive_length`), extrude's two sides included (a sum below `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` is `BothSidesZero`). Revolve checks that the axis lies in the sketch plane and that the profile stays on one side of it on the sketch regions, before any kernel work, treats an angle at most 1e-9 rad as out of range like 0 and one within 1e-9 rad of 360 deg as a full turn. Hole lives in `builtin/hole/`: `mod.rs` holds the feature and its build steps (`distinct_points`, `drill_candidates`, `finish`), `size.rs` the checked sizes and the half cross-section, `tool.rs` the segment roles (their discriminants are the curve tags) and the tool, and `overlap.rs` the overlap decision (`cut_candidates`, `tools_with_own_faces`, `shared_volumes`). It evaluates and checks the diameter (its radius at least `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, since the entry and bottom segments are that long), then the depth, then the head (a head diameter at least D + 2 `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, a counterbore depth at least `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`), then the tip, and requires a head to end at least `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` above the shoulder (`ends_above_shoulder`), reporting the bounds the user typed (the depth, or the head diameter whose cone ends at it). It builds one tool per distinct point by revolving a half cross-section in the plane of the sketch x axis and the drilling direction, its segments tagged by role (`entry`, `counterbore`, `counterbore-floor`, `countersink`, `bore`, `bottom`, `tip`, and the axis segment, which sweeps no face); a point whose tool does not overlap the body gets `HoleMissesBody`. The body's bounding box is read once; a tool whose box misses it does not overlap, and the others are cut from the body with one kernel `cut` (`cut_candidates`). A cut that leaves the volume unchanged means no tool overlaps; otherwise a tool overlaps when the cut's history has a result face from one of its faces and from no body face (`tools_with_own_faces`), and a tool without one (it misses, touches or swallows whole solids) is decided by its shared volume (`shared_volumes`: one kernel `common` with the compound of such tools when their boxes are more than `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` apart, each solid of the result counting for the tool whose box is nearest to its center, else one `common` per tool). When every cut tool leaves a face of its own, the cut goes straight through `apply_op_result` (whose `NoEffect` is the unchanged volume) and is the result, so the usual hole costs one boolean on the body; otherwise the cut's volume and the shared volumes decide the other tools before anything is named, the cut goes through `apply_op_result` only when every tool overlaps, and else the overlapping tools are cut again with `apply_op`. A through-all hole is max(F, h) + 1 mm long, with F the farthest corner distance and h the head depth, so a head deeper than the body still leaves a bore below it. Each overlapping tool whose end face (`bottom`, or `tip` with a tip) the result lacks is recorded absent (`ToolEnds`, `absent_ends`): `Through` when its `entry` is gone too, else `EndOutside`.
- Dress-up (features/fillet.md, chamfer.md, shell.md): `fillet` (`Fillet`), `chamfer` (`Chamfer`, with `ChamferDistance` tagged `equal` or `two_distances`) and `shell` (`Shell`). They share `dressup.rs`: fillet and chamfer call its steps in order (`select_edges`, `no_effect` when no edge is left, `sorted_by_name`, `run_or_search`, then `finish_blend`, which names the result and makes the update); `select_edges` resolves `edges` and skips smooth edges with an `Ignored` warning (fewer than two faces in the body state's topology, as at a seam, or two faces that kernel `Shape::edge_normals` records as `G1OrBetter` or whose outward normals at the edge's middle differ by at most `SMOOTH_ANGLE`, 1e-3 rad, on every surface type; a knife edge, with opposite normals, and an edge the query rejects as invalid input, where a face has no defined normal, have a corner), `run_or_search` calls the kernel and runs the "too large" search on any failure except invalid input, and `add_origins` turns kernel history into the face origins that `name_blend` and the shell's naming map to names (naming.md sections 3.6.5 to 3.6.7), and `modified` makes the update, which carries the topology the naming read. Fillet and chamfer pass their edges to the kernel sorted by element name in byte order (`dressup::sorted_by_name`), so the order of `edges` never matters (the kernel's blend surfaces, down to the last bits, and a chamfer's side of a tangent chain depend on the order it gets edges in), and a two-distance chamfer measures `expr` on the face of each edge whose name sorts first (`flip` swaps the distances). Shell hollows each solid of the body with its own removed faces (a solid without any becomes a closed hollow) and combines several results with the kernel's `compound`, mapping each solid's history through `Shape::locate`.
- Body features (features/boolean.md, mirror.md, linear_pattern.md, circular_pattern.md, move_body.md, split_body.md, import.md): `boolean` (`Boolean`, with `BooleanOp`), `mirror` (`Mirror`), `linear_pattern` (`LinearPattern`, with `LinearPatternDirection`), `circular_pattern` (`CircularPattern`), `move_body` (`MoveBody`, with `MoveBodyRotation`), `split_body` (`SplitBody`) and `import` (`Import`). `boolean` checks the target and every tool body for a solid, then calls `apply_op` with one `Tool` per tool body (its shape and a copy of its naming map), which makes one kernel `fuse`, `cut` or `common` call; consumed tools get `Clear` updates even when the operation had no effect. The copy features share `copy.rs`: `validate_target` and `add_source`/`add_target` for `op` and `target`, `op_hint`, `check_instance_limit` (at most 10000 instances, the original included; patterns read their counts with `eval_count(..., 1)`, and check `common::check_canceled` between copies), and `Source` (`new` checks the source holds a solid), whose `copy` transforms the source and names it with `NamingMap::with_instance` and whose `place` combines every copy with one `apply_op` (join into the source, new into the target). With op new a target that holds a solid is `BodyNotEmpty` at any count, with or without copies; without copies (count 1) the source keeps an unchanged `Join` update, or the still empty target gets none, with a `NoEffect` warning. `move_body` carries the input naming map over unchanged and reports `NoEffect` with an unchanged `Modify` update when nothing moves. `split_body` needs a solid above `VOLUME_TOLERANCE` of the body's volume on each side, names the whole kernel split once (section faces by side, other faces by history) and gives each body the projection of that map onto its solids through `Shape::locate`. `import` names every face `<id>/face` (numbered `#n` in geometric order) and has no dedicated Python form. Nested param structs use `deny_unknown_fields` so an unknown key inside them is reported instead of dropped.

## Rules

- Built-in features use only the public API, like a plugin would (features.md section 2.11): `?` on kernel calls (`From<KernelError>`), `FeatureError::internal`, `point`, `vector`, `BuildContext::face_placement`, `VOLUME_TOLERANCE`, `apply_op_result`, `Measurements`, `BodyState::measurements` and the naming bridge are public for that, and items of private modules are imported from the crate root (`crate::name_shape`, `crate::PrimitivePlacement`). `builtin/common.rs` holds what the built-ins share and uses only public items, including `positive_length` and `is_positive_length` (the positive length rule of section 3.4, which fillet, chamfer and shell use too), `length_in_range` and `angle_in_range` (a value checked right after it is evaluated), `out_of_range` (an `OutOfRange` whose message bound differs from the rule's), `require_selection` (`EmptySelection` in `validate`), `check_canceled`, `three_lengths`, `single_or_compound`, `swept_tool`, `profile_messages`, `target_hint`, `naming_failed` (a too long name as `NameTooDeep`, any other `NamingError` as `Internal`) and `unchanged` (an update that keeps a body's state and its measurements). The framework's own long steps use the crate-private `BuildContext::check_canceled`.
- Every registered feature needs an example in `examples/`.
- `FeatureError` keeps the payload types the spec gives, so the crate expects `clippy::result_large_err` (see `lib.rs`).
