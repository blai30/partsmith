# M2 wave 2 spec round: decisions of 2026-11-19

The lead of M2 wave 2's spec round made these decisions on 2026-11-19, before the round's tracks wrote their specs and ADRs. Wave 2's ADRs and notes cite them as "the wave 2 decisions of 2026-11-19" by section and item, and this record keeps that numbering. Section and test numbers are those of 2026-11-19. Every choice below is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05): the most open-source approach, then the safest, then the industry standard, then the recommendation of the work (the owner's standing rule in [the ADR log](../adr/README.md)). Each ADR that a decision feeds states the options, the pick and the rule's reasons in that order, and marks what needs human review at the M2 gate ([m2-plan.md](m2-plan.md) gate 19).

## 2. The critic's findings

The round's critic reviewed the plan. All its findings are accepted:

1. Stage 1 specifies only measured constructions. Up-to targets are planar and cylindrical faces only. Every other surface kind, cones included, is refused with a message that names the surface kind. Up-to and taper on a profile that holds a conic or B-spline curve, or on more than one region, are refused with messages of their own. The never-reached and partly-reached checks stay (kernel.md section 8.5). Track 4A widens these cases from spike 8.10's recorded Results: features/extrude.md joins 4A's files, with tests from 126.
2. A piece of a split conic or spline (the planner's choice "How a piece of a split conic or spline is represented") takes option (a): the whole curve plus a parameter range. kernel.md section 2.5 gains the range. `PlanarCurve` carries an optional parameter range, built in the shim as a `Geom2d_TrimmedCurve` over the curve and checked against its neighbors within `LINEAR`. Track 1B writes this in kernel.md section 2.5 with its bridge data and tests. Track 1A writes the ADR and sketch.md section 2.7 to match. Neither waits for the other: this decision fixes the contract.
3. Shared lists have one writer. The integrator who closes a stage writes file-format.md section 3.9's version 3 row and protocol.md section 1's list of M2 selection kinds from each track's hand-off. Track 1C adds the `vertex` noun to engine.md section 3.22. Track 2C adds the `vertex` placeholder to ui.md section 3.7. ADR 0066 decision 2 joins the amended decisions and gate 19's list.
4. Wave 2's spikes (kernel.md sections 8.8 to 8.10) have their criteria committed before any spike commit (ADR 0071 alternative 2). Each spike branches from the commit that holds its criteria, stays local, and is recorded as `docs/design/spikes/<name>.patch` and `.json`. Size deltas are measured against the core at that base, with OCCT built at `-Oz` (ADR 0076). Track 1B adds dated notes to kernel.md section 8.1. The stage 1 integrator adds dated notes to ADR 0071 and m2-plan.md: item 10's spikes now also run in wave 2, a departure on gate 19's list.
5. Threads model the maximum-material limit of their class: ISO 965-1's fundamental deviations (6g's `es`, and `EI = 0` for 6H) and ASME B1.1's allowance (2A; 2B has none). They are computed by the standards' formulas, with tests that check them against published values. Tolerance widths are not modeled or stored. On top come the FDM clearance per side (`printer.fit.slip`, the planner's choice "How printer.fit reaches threads and holes") and an explicit crest and root truncation that does not depend on the technology. The truncation is a stated fraction of `P` beyond ISO 68-1's flats, chosen in track 2D's ADR with its source (technical design section 11: "enlarged clearance, truncated crests"), with tests. Spike 8.9 uses the spike profile of kernel.md section 8.2. Track 3A checks it again against 2D's profile.
6. The coil keeps m2-plan.md item 19's taper: a taper angle (the cone's parameter plane; kernel.md section 8.2 measured a 5-degree cone) and a right or left hand, in features/coil.md and in spike 8.8's inputs.
7. The table route. Track 3A owns protocol.md in stage 3. It writes the additive manifest member (the `FieldHint` member that names a core table) and the route that carries a table's rows to the UI and Python, with tests, under the protocol review. Track 2D describes the tables, and what a host needs from them, in features/thread.md. It writes nothing in protocol.md.
8. ADR numbers. Tracks write new ADRs under placeholder ids of the form `ADR W2-<track>-<n>`, in files named `docs/adr/W2-<track>-<n>-<slug>.md`. The integrator who closes a stage assigns final numbers from 0077 upward in track order (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, then 2A and on), renames the files and rewrites every citation. Numbers are never changed later to close a gap. Stage 1's ADRs became 0077 to 0093.
9. Newcomer error corpus scenario NC15 belongs to the thread (track 3A), and NC16 to the sweep (track 4A). NC17 and NC18 stay reserved for wave 3.
10. Each UI track adds its E-160 rows, and that stage's owner of delivery.md adds them to test 71's list. A runner check that every `SketchRecipe` variant has a tool example joins delivery.md section 2.8 (a note to ADR 0051, by track 1D). The tests labeled "item 24" that item 14's route takes are labeled again as item 14's, by tracks 1A and 2A. Track 1D updates delivery.md section 2.8's twin rule for imports.
11. The changes by spec first that the critic listed join their tracks: corpus.md test 8 (track 1C, with a brackets model that measures an up-to reference), features/extrude.md test 64 (1C), features/hole.md test 41 (3A), sketch.md test 255 (1A), and protocol.md tests 34 and 71 (1A).

The plan's new deferrals (arc length on elliptical arcs and splines, a start from faces that are not planar, two-sided tapers, and cones and up-to on several regions until track 4A) are routed by the stage 1 integrator in a dated note in m2-plan.md section 5.

## 3. The open choices

The planner listed 65 open choices, each with its options and a recommendation. All are decided as the planner recommends, except five:

- "Targets that up-to accepts, which body, and a start inside the target": planes and cylinders only in stage 1 (section 2 item 1).
- "Which profile is modeled, and the FDM truncation": section 2 item 5.
- "Coil feature types, curve output, modes, profile and frame": the recommendation, plus a taper and a hand (section 2 item 6).
- "How a thread size is typed in params, the manifest and the inspector": the `FieldHint` member and the table route move to track 3A (section 2 item 7). The rest stands: a standard enum, plus a size string checked against the core's table with a fix that offers the nearest match.
- "Head sizes for Hole.counterbore(M3), and inch clearance holes": the recommendation stands (explicit head sizes in M2; per-screw head presets and ASME B18.2.8 clearance holes go to M3). It is recorded as a scope deferral, with a dated note in m2-plan.md, and is on gate 19's list.

Where an ADR departs from a recommendation for a reason found while writing it, the ADR records the departure and its reason (ADR 0078 alternative 2, ADR 0091 alternative 5, and ADR 0093 alternatives 3 and 4).

### Choices cited by name

Wave 2's records cite these choices by name. Each is listed with its options, the planner's recommendation and the ADR that decides it:

- "Point on and tangent to a conic or spline in the solver" ([ADR 0077](../adr/0077-solver-rows-of-conics-and-splines.md)). Options: (a) a hidden curve parameter per `point_on_object` or `tangent` on a spline, warm-started by projection and never stored, with implicit rows on the ellipse, parabola, hyperbola and conic; (b) a foot-point residual with an inner Newton solve; (c) splines take only coincidences and tangents at their ends in M2. Recommended: (a), with implicit rows scaled to mm.
- "Property generator for the new kinds and item 15's constraints" (ADR 0077). Options: (a) a separate generator; (b) extending `tests/common/generator.rs`. Recommended: (a).
- "Fit-spline interpolation" ([ADR 0078](../adr/0078-fit-spline-interpolation-and-spline-tool-defaults.md)). Options: parameters by (a) chord length, (b) centripetal or (c) uniform spacing; ends by (i) not-a-knot, (ii) natural or (iii) Bessel tangents; a periodic cubic for periodic splines. Recommended: chord-length parameters with knots from them, not-a-knot ends and the periodic cubic, checked against OCCT's `Geom2dAPI_Interpolate` before writing. That check found OCCT's own end rule, which ADR 0078 takes.
- "Defaults of the control-point spline tool" (ADR 0078). Options: (a) degree `min(3, poles - 1)` with clamped uniform knots; (b) a degree the user picks in the tool. For periodic splines, a toggle or a close on the first pole. Recommended: (a), with a periodic toggle.
- "Intersection method of the arrangement for new kinds" ([ADR 0079](../adr/0079-conic-and-spline-pieces-intersections-areas-and-lengths.md)). Options: (a) algebraic for line-conic and conic-conic pairs, subdivision for splines; (b) every new kind as rational Bezier spans, with subdivision and Newton polishing; (c) intersect the polylines, then refine. Recommended: (b), with fixed iteration counts and stated tolerances, and M1's closed forms kept for lines and circles.
- "A spline that crosses itself" (ADR 0079). Options: (i) split it at its own crossings and report `self_intersection` with `[a, a]`; (ii) a new finding that drops the spline; (iii) no support. Recommended: (i).
- "How a piece of a split conic or spline is represented" (ADR 0079 and [ADR 0082](../adr/0082-conic-and-spline-profile-curves-in-the-kernel.md)). Options: (a) the whole curve plus a parameter range, trimmed in the kernel; (b) in-house knot insertion; (c) the end points only. Recommended: (a), with vertices computed once and shared bitwise. Section 2 item 2 fixes the kernel's half.
- "How item 14's drawing tools reach the core in wave 2" ([ADR 0080](../adr/0080-conic-and-spline-tools-reach-the-core-in-wave-2.md)). ADR 0066 decision 11 and protocol.md command 39 gave the route to item 24 in wave 3. Options: (a) item 14 lands `apply_recipe`, command 39 and `sketch_operation_failed` with its own variants, and item 24 adds M1's tools in wave 3; (b) the conic and spline tools wait for wave 3; (c) recipes in TypeScript. Recommended: (a), by an ADR that amends ADR 0066 decision 11.
- "Live previews of conic and spline tools without curve evaluation in the UI" (ADR 0080). Options: (a) command 39 with a candidate, then a solve, through `LatestOnly` on each pointer move; (b) a preview option that returns polylines; (c) the scaffold only; (d) curve evaluation in the UI, which amends D15. Recommended: (a). The scaffold draws at once, and the curve when the answer arrives.
- "Handles and dimensions for the axes of ellipses and hyperbolas" ([ADR 0081](../adr/0081-axis-roles-of-ellipses-and-hyperbolas.md)). Options: (a) the point roles `major` and `minor` at the ends of the semi-axes; (b) construction axis lines tied by internal-alignment constraints; (c) an axis key on radius and distance; (d) none in M2. Recommended: (a).
- "Which new roles count as hole points and sketch_point picks" (ADR 0081). Options: (a) every new role; (b) M1's list only; (c) the centers of ellipses and elliptical arcs and the ends of every open kind, but no vertex, focus, control point or axis end. Recommended: (c).
- "Revolve two sides and symmetric in the kernel" ([ADR 0083](../adr/0083-extrusions-that-end-on-a-face-or-a-shape-and-revolutions-between-two-angles.md)). Options: (a) the revolve takes a start and an end angle; (b) the feature rotates the profile's plane and rebuilds the profile. Recommended: (a).
- "Glyph offset and label width under choice 22 (b)" ([ADR 0090](../adr/0090-sketch-glyphs-and-dimension-labels-get-24-pixel-targets.md)). Options: keep the 14-pixel offset, or grow it until a button clears the pick bands; labels with `min-h-6` only, or with `min-h-6 min-w-6`. Recommended: an offset of at least 20 pixels (12 + 7.5), a point glyph's offset that clears the point's 9.5-pixel pick radius, and labels with `min-h-6 min-w-6`.
- "A download Save of a document holding picked assets" ([ADR 0092](../adr/0092-a-download-save-also-downloads-the-imported-files.md), ui.md open issue 45). Options: (i) download each asset too; (ii) refuse with a warning; (iii) offer Import only with the File System Access API or on the desktop; (iv) warn at import. Recommended: (i), with a message that names the assets folder.
- "How printer.fit reaches threads and holes, and which class applies" (track 2D, section 2 item 5). Options: (i) a clearance expression whose start value is `printer.fit.slip`, which amends ADR 0065; (ii) a fit-class field plus a new `Dependencies` member; (iii) a fixed number as the start value. The class is slip, snug or one per hole, and ISO 273 holes may take it too. Recommended: (i), with `printer.fit.slip` applied per side to the thread's major and minor diameters and to ISO 273 holes, and the user free to set 0. Section 2 item 13 keeps features.md test 54 as it is.
- "Detecting a boolean that dropped an argument" (track 3A, stage 3; [ADR 0084](../adr/0084-wrap-curves-and-modeled-threads-in-the-kernel.md) puts the check in the kernel). Options: (i) tests only; (ii) a build-time check of solids and volume; (iii) a check after every boolean of the facade. Recommended: (ii), failing with a message that offers the cosmetic thread.
