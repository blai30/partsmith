# 0083: Extrusions that end on a face or a shape, and revolutions between two angles

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05); needs human review at the M2 gate for its change to the kernel facade's public API, the rule of [ADR 0026](0026-kernel-facade-additions-for-errors.md): the call `extrude_to` with `ExtrudeEnd`, `revolve`'s new start angle (every caller changes), `Operation::ExtrudeTo`, and the variants `KernelErrorKind::TargetNotReached`, `TargetPartlyReached` and `TargetStartsInside` and `SweepFace::Target`. Code outside `partsmith-occt-sys` may match on these variants, as [ADR 0026](0026-kernel-facade-additions-for-errors.md) and [ADR 0053](0053-copy-features-refuse-a-body-that-is-not-valid.md) added kinds before. It specifies the kernel calls of the M2 plan's item 17 in kernel.md sections 2.6, 3.4.3 and 3.4.4 and settles the plan's choice "Revolve two sides and symmetric in the kernel". It changes no file format, protocol schema or naming algorithm and adds no dependency: `BRepAlgoAPI_Splitter` is in `TKBO`, `BRepOffsetAPI_DraftAngle` in `TKOffset` and `BRepClass_FaceClassifier` in `TKTopAlgo`, all linked (kernel.md section 3.12 item 3).

## Context

- **Spike 8.5** (kernel.md section 8.5, 2026-11-16) picked the split prism for an extrude up to a face or a body: `BRepPrimAPI_MakePrism` through the target, split by `BRepAlgoAPI_Splitter` at the face's surface or at the body, the pieces on the profile's side kept. It picked `BRepOffsetAPI_DraftAngle` on the prism's lateral faces for a taper, split the same way up to a face. It measured them on profiles of lines, arcs and circles extruded along their plane's normal, up to planes, a tilted plane, a cylinder and a box, with tapers of 5 degrees and a taper up to a tilted plane. It found that OCCT reports no status for a face the profile reaches only in part. The shim's own check is the status. It did not measure cones, start faces, steep inward tapers, oblique directions, profiles of several regions or of conics and splines, or revolve up to a face.
- **The decisions of 2026-11-19** (wave 2's spec round, item 1) let stage 1 specify only measured constructions: planar and cylindrical targets, with every other surface kind refused by a message that names it, and up-to and tapers on profiles of conics, splines or several regions refused with messages of their own. Spike 8.10's criteria (kernel.md section 8.10) are written now, and track 4A widens from its Results.
- **Features need to tell the failures apart.** features/extrude.md (track 1C) words a target never reached and one reached in part as the newcomer error NC14, with different fixes. A kernel `InvalidInput` reaches the user only as "likely a bug" (kernel.md section 4.3), and features never parse the kernel's sentences.
- **Revolve** took one angle from the profile's position. Two-sided and symmetric revolutions (item 17) need a start other than the profile.
- **The rib** (item 28, wave 3) extrudes a face that `make_profile` does not build up to the body, along the face's normal (kernel.md section 8.6 reused spike 8.5's `up_to_body` for it). Spike 8.6's `inside` rib, whose profile runs 3 mm into each plate, starts partly inside the body. The spike's split had no start check and kept the pieces inside, and the fuse that followed absorbed them.

## Decision

1. **One call for every end.** `extrude_to(profile, direction, start, end, taper, interrupt)` with `ExtrudeEnd::Offset`, `Face { shape, faces }` or `Shape(shape)`, and a taper in radians, positive when the section grows along the direction. `extrude` stays, as the case of an offset with no taper: same checks, construction and bytes. `extrude_to` reads a profile's shape, plane and edge tags, never its regions. Item 28's thickened face extrudes through it, with the face's own plane as its `plane`, whose normal is the rib's direction. Up to a shape, a profile that starts partly inside it is refused (decision 3), and a rib profile that ends inside its body is one: item 28's spec trims that profile to the body first or adds a kernel option that skips the start check (kernel.md open issues 21 (e) and 23). A `Face` end is a list of faces that lie on one plane or one cylinder: a face reference can resolve to several pieces of a split face, and [ADR 0086](0086-up-to-targets-direction-and-reach-checks.md) decision 1 ends the extrusion on their union.
2. **Spike 8.5's constructions, and only what it measured.** The split prism up to a planar or cylindrical face or up to a shape, along the plane's normal either way, and `DraftAngle` over an offset or up to a planar face. Everything else is refused with its own message (kernel.md section 3.4.3 item 1, open issue 23) until spike 8.10's Results widen it. An oblique direction up to a face or a shape is refused too: features/extrude.md leaves oblique extrusions to M3.
3. **Reach checks with kinds of their own.** Before the prism, the shim refuses a profile with a sample inside the shape it runs up to (`TargetStartsInside`), as [ADR 0086](0086-up-to-targets-direction-and-reach-checks.md) decisions 4 and 7 ask of the kernel: the split would keep the piece from the profile to where it leaves the body. After the split, the shim refuses a result that holds no image of the target (`TargetNotReached`), a face target whose end samples all lie outside its faces (`TargetNotReached`), a kept piece that still holds the through prism's far cap, and a face target with some samples outside its faces (`TargetPartlyReached`). A sample is outside when every target face puts it outside. Their messages name no face index.
4. **Roles from history.** The face where an extrusion up to a face ends is the image of the cutting face, with role `EndCap`. An extrusion up to a shape ends on pieces of that shape's faces, each with `SweepFace::Target { face }`. Lateral faces and the start cap keep `extrude`'s roles. Edges and vertices are named by their faces, as for `extrude`.
5. **Revolve between two angles.** `revolve(profile, axis, start, end, interrupt)`: the shim rotates the profile to `start` (a location) and revolves it by `end - start`. The plan's option (a). M1's calls become `revolve(profile, axis, 0.0, angle, ..)` with the same bytes.
6. **No lock.** `BRepAlgoAPI_Splitter` and `DraftAngle` run without a mutex. Test 223 runs them on 8 threads. A difference would bring a mutex and an open issue.

## Alternatives considered

Every option is open source, built on OCCT. The first criterion of the rule picks none of them. The rule then takes the safest, then the industry standard, then this work's recommendation. Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05).

1. *The call's shape.* Options: (i) a new `extrude_to` beside `extrude`; (ii) `extrude` takes the end and the taper, and every M1 caller changes; (iii) one call per end kind. The rule takes (i), the safest: M1's `extrude` and its 6 tests stay as they are, and one construction serves both calls. (iii) would repeat the taper and the checks three times.
2. *How a feature learns why the target was missed.* Options: (i) error kinds of their own; (ii) an `Ok` result with a reach status beside the shape; (iii) `InvalidInput` with sentences the feature compares. The rule takes (i), the safest: a missed target never returns geometry that a caller could use by mistake, and the feature matches a kind, as it does for `Canceled`. (ii) returns the whole through prism or a piece past the target as a result. (iii) ties features to the kernel's wording. Kinds are also the pattern of ADR 0026 and ADR 0053.
3. *What stage 1 takes* (decided by the decisions of 2026-11-19, item 1). Options: (i) only the measured constructions, the rest refused until spike 8.10; (ii) cones and the rest "if spike 8.10 passes". The rule takes (i), the safest: a test author gets one fixed behavior, and a spike result cannot reach code before its criteria were judged. Oblique directions up to a face or a shape fall under (i). Their through length would also need the profile's own extent along the direction, which spike 8.5 never measured.
4. *Revolve two sides* (the plan's choice). Options: (a) the kernel takes a start and an end angle; (b) the feature rotates the profile's plane and builds the profile again. The rule takes (a), the safest: it builds no profile twice and keeps the roles of `SweepFace`, where (b) runs `make_profile` again on every recompute and can round the profile's points. It also mirrors `extrude`'s start and end.
5. *The taper's sign.* Options: (i) positive when the section grows along the direction; (ii) positive when it shrinks. Both are equally safe, and the feature maps its own convention to either (features/extrude.md, [ADR 0087](0087-taper.md)). The industry standard does not decide here, since the feature and not the kernel sets the user's sign. This work recommends (i), spike 8.5's sign, under which its measured volumes and test 218's hold.
6. *One target face or several.* Options: (i) a list of faces on one plane or one cylinder; (ii) one face, with the feature calling once per face of its reference; (iii) one face, with the feature refusing a reference that resolves to several. The rule takes (i), the safest: the reach checks see the whole target. A profile that spans two pieces of a split face is reached, where (ii) reports it as reached in part on each piece and (iii) refuses a reference ADR 0086 takes.

## Consequences

- kernel.md sections 2.6, 2.13, 3.4.3, 3.4.4, 3.9 item 4, 3.10 item 9, 3.13, 4, 5 and tests 211 to 227 state it. `Operation` gains `ExtrudeTo`.
- features/extrude.md and features/revolve.md (track 1C) build on these calls. They refuse what kernel.md section 3.4.3 item 1 refuses with their own messages first, map `TargetNotReached` and `TargetPartlyReached` to NC14's messages and fixes, and map `TargetStartsInside` to `up_to_starts_inside`. A profile on the boundary of the shape it runs up to is spike 8.10's input.
- Track 4A widens section 3.4.3 item 1 and features/extrude.md from spike 8.10's Results, or an ADR keeps a class refused (open issue 23). Revolve up to a face waits for the same spike.
- A rib profile that ends inside its body is refused up to the body with `TargetStartsInside`. features/rib.md (item 28, wave 3) trims the profile to the body first or specifies a kernel option that skips the start check (kernel.md open issue 21 (e)).
- No check refuses an inward taper whose lateral faces meet before the end. features/extrude.md test 107 expects it to fail in `DraftAngle` or in the validity check. Spike 8.5 did not run such a taper. Spike 8.10 runs test 107's input, and a valid solid there brings a shim check before that test is coded (kernel.md section 3.4.3's closing paragraph).
- The section 5 rows of `extrude_to` are set in wave 4 from measured medians ([ADR 0057](0057-budgets-set-from-the-measured-median-plus-20-percent.md)). `DraftAngle` never polls inside, and its cancel latency is measured and recorded in open issue 17.
- The core size of the change is recorded (kernel.md section 5, [ADR 0076](0076-occt-built-for-the-web-at-oz.md) decision 6).
