# 0057: Budgets set from the measured median plus 20 percent

Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-11-05): the full-rebuild budget the `macos-15` runner holds, set from its first median (ADR 0054 decision 6, option (a)), and the rows over on the 4-core container revised to what is measured (kernel.md open issue 10, features.md open issues 5 and 18, model.md open issue 9 and engine.md open issue 6). The margin, its rounding and the other choices below are Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05). Choice 4 was revised on 2026-11-06 after review: the cold open first took the full rebuild's budget, with no margin left. No schema, protocol, public API, license, dependency or capability change: only budget rows, the bodies two benchmark rows time and the docs that state them change.

## Context

- **The first runner median.** Technical design section 14's full rebuild of a 50-feature part has 1 s on the desktop. Since ADR 0054 the job `full-rebuild` of `platforms.yml` times it on `macos-15`. Its first median was 1.477 s (platforms run 14, 41926898084, on 0ae7337). The owner chose option (a) of ADR 0054 decision 6: a budget that the runner holds, from that median, with a stated margin for runner noise. Speed work is later work.
- **Rows over on the container.** The owner also decided that the rows over on the 4-core development container follow what is measured: `extrude` of a 100-edge profile (kernel.md section 5, 15 ms), ten holes in the 194-hole plate (features.md section 5, 80 ms), the shell near a round's radius as a row of its own of 250 ms (features.md open issue 18), an insertion at the start of a 1,000-feature document as a row of its own of 100 µs (model.md open issue 9), and in engine.md section 5 the first `list_elements` (10 ms), `analyze_print` (300 ms), timed until now on a 500 mm sphere taller than any printer, and the cold `open_document` (1 s). The cold open follows the full-rebuild decision.
- **What the other budgets use.** No budget of the repository was set from a measurement with a stated margin. Technical design section 16 blocks a regression over 10 percent on fixed hardware runners. Those runners do not exist yet (delivery.md 1.2). The web columns are 3 times the desktop budgets.
- **Noise.** A hosted runner is a virtual machine whose speed varies between runs and runner images (ADR 0054). There was one runner median on 2026-11-05. On the container, the medians of one build's runs of `run of bench50 from an empty cache` spread by 3 percent on 2026-11-05 (2.179 to 2.245 s, five runs), 13 percent on 2026-11-04 (1.676 to 1.892 s, four runs) and 19 percent on 2026-10-16 (2.556 to 3.033 s, four runs at low load). The first `list_elements` read 17.7 to 21.4 ms on 2026-11-05 and 24.5 to 36.1 ms on 2026-10-16.

## Decision

1. **The margin rule.** A budget set from a measurement is the median measured plus 20 percent, rounded up to two significant digits. Where the owner named a number, that number is built when it is at least the median plus 20 percent. Otherwise the rule's number is built. Rows measured on the container use the median of three runs of the release benchmark or release test, each run the row's own median.
2. **The full rebuild.** 1.477 s plus 20 percent is 1.772 s. Rounded up, that is 1.8 s. Technical design section 14's desktop budget of a full rebuild is 1.8 s, judged on `macos-15`. The web's 3 s stays. recompute.md section 5's row and the row `run of bench50 from an empty cache` of `crates/partsmith-recompute/benches/budgets.rs` read 1.8 s. `full-rebuild`'s summary shows the budget `1.800s`. The release checklist asks for the median within this budget, or for an ADR the owner decided that covers it (delivery.md 3.6, ADR 0054 decision 6). Reusing the fingerprints of kept faces through the kernel's history (recompute.md open issue 4) is recorded as later work and not scheduled.

## Options considered

The owner decided the full-rebuild budget's form and the rows to revise. **The choices the owner's rule settles.** Each is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05).

1. *The margin.* The options: (i) 10 percent, technical design section 16's regression threshold; (ii) 20 percent; (iii) a statistical margin, such as the median plus three median absolute deviations of many runs. The rule takes (ii), the industry practice for budgets on shared runners: a fixed percentage over a measured baseline. It covers the spreads measured here between runs of one build (3, 13 and 19 percent). It is twice the 10 percent threshold. Under (i) noise alone would warn. (iii) needs many runner medians. There was one.
2. *The rounding.* The options: (i) none, 1.772 s; (ii) up to two significant digits, 1.8 s; (iii) up to the next round step, 2 s. The rule takes (ii), the least slack that still gives a number a person reads at a glance.
3. *A number the owner named.* The options: (i) the owner's number as written; (ii) the rule's number; (iii) the owner's number when it leaves the margin, else the rule's. The rule takes (iii), the safest: the owner's number is built where it holds. No budget set from a measurement sits closer to its median than 20 percent. Only the hole row differs: about 300 ms became 320 ms.
4. *How the cold open follows the full rebuild.* The options: (i) the same budget, as the two rows had at 1 s; (ii) the full rebuild's budget times the open's share, the meshing and encoding the open adds to the rebuild; (iii) a budget from the container's own median. The rule takes (ii), the safest. Three alternating runs of each on the container read 2.451, 2.453 and 2.480 s for the cold open and 2.070, 2.068 and 2.131 s for `bench50` from an empty cache. The ratio of the medians is 1.185, and 1.8 s times 1.185 is 2.13 s, rounded up to 2.2 s. The first build took (i). A review showed that under (i) a machine whose full rebuild meets its budget with decision 1's margin reads the cold open at its budget, with no margin left (1.477 s times 1.185 is 1.75 s against 1.8 s). Under (iii) the container would set a budget for the reference laptop. The cold open stays over on the container (2.45 s against 2.2 s), as the full rebuild does (2.07 s against 1.8 s). The container runs `bench50` 1.4 to 1.5 times slower than the runner.
5. *The body that fits the printer.* The options: (i) a plate of 5 x 5 knobs, spheres of radius 5 mm on a 2 mm plate, for `generic-resin` (194,287 triangles, 10 mm, 200 layers); (ii) the batch of 20 balls of decision 4; (iii) an imported mesh (M1 imports no meshes). The rule takes (ii), the recommendation: it is the input print.md section 5 derives its budget from. The engine's row then stays comparable with the print crate's. The knob plate read 3.36 to 3.46 s. A profile put 67 percent of its time in `thin_walls`, most likely in the bounding volume hierarchy's boxes of the plate's long flat triangles. That is recorded as later work in print.md open issue 9.
6. *The web column.* The options: (i) unchanged, with two web budgets under their desktop budgets; (ii) each changed row's old ratio to its desktop budget; (iii) 3 times the desktop budget where a change is needed; (iv) Chromium medians from the container plus the margin. The rule takes (iii), the ratio technical design section 14 and the crate specs state. Under (ii) the ratios differ by row. engine.md open issue 19 says runs off the reference machine decide no web budget. That rules out (iv).
7. *The repeated `list_elements`.* The options: (i) keep 10 ms; (ii) the margin rule, 12 ms. The rule takes (i), the safest: the owner's decision revised the first call only. The repeated row is within its budget here. It sits 1 to 9 percent under it (9.15 to 9.9 ms), less than decision 1's margin. A busy container can read it over. Its later work is engine.md open issue 6's encoding.
