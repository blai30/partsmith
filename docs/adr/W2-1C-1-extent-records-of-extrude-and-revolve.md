# W2-1C-1: Extent records of extrude and revolve

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05); needs human review at the M2 gate: it adds values and keys to format version 3 (decisions 1 to 5, [ADR 0061](0061-format-version-3-for-the-records-of-m2.md)). It settles the record shape of the M2 plan's item 17 for features/extrude.md and features/revolve.md. Revolve up to a face is not part of it: spike 8.10 measures its construction in stage 2, and track 4A writes its record in stage 4.

## Context

- **Item 17.** Extrude gains up to a face, a body or a vertex, a start from an offset or a face, through all in both directions, and a taper. Revolve gains two sides and symmetric (m2-plan.md section 2.1 item 17). Up to a face for revolve waits for its spike.
- **M1's records.** `ExtrudeExtent` is tagged by `kind` with `distance`, `symmetric`, `two_sides` and `through_all`, and denies unknown keys inside the extent. `RevolveExtent` has `angle` and `full` (`crates/partsmith-features/src/builtin/extrude.rs` and `revolve.rs`). The params themselves keep unknown keys, and features.md tests 8 and 9 and recompute.md test 15 use `taper` as such a key at the top level.
- **The rules of version 3.** ADR 0061 decision 3: a new value of a tagged enum, a new optional key omitted when absent, or a new record type, each listed in file-format.md section 3.9's version 3 row. ADR 0069 rule 3: a face or vertex reference sits beside a `body` field in the same object.
- **Fusion.** An extrude has a start (profile plane, offset, object), one or two sides, each with its extent and its taper. A revolve has one side, two sides or symmetric, each an angle or "To".

## Decision

1. **Flat one-sided kinds.** `ExtrudeExtent` gains four values after `through_all`, in this order: `through_all_both` (no keys), `up_to_face` (`body`, `face`), `up_to_body` (`target`) and `up_to_vertex` (`body`, `vertex`). The face and the vertex sit beside their `body` (ADR 0069 rule 3). `up_to_body` names its body `target`: the extent's `body` would be the body of a face in the other two kinds, and the inspector shows one field per path. M1's four values keep their shape.
2. **The taper lives in the extent.** `distance`, `through_all`, `up_to_face`, `up_to_body` and `up_to_vertex` gain the optional key `taper`, an angle expression, last in the object and omitted when absent ([ADR W2-1C-3](W2-1C-3-taper.md)). `symmetric`, `two_sides` and `through_all_both` have no `taper` key, so their schema refuses one.
3. **The start is a top-level key.** `ExtrudeParams` gains `start` between `profile` and `extent`, an `ExtrudeStart` tagged by `kind`: `sketch_plane` (the default, omitted when it is the value), `offset` (`expr`, a length) and `face` (`body`, `face`). An offset is measured along the sketch normal, whatever `reverse` says. A face start takes a flat face parallel to the sketch plane and starts on its plane. `through_all_both` refuses every start but `sketch_plane`.
4. **Revolve two sides and symmetric.** `RevolveExtent` gains `two_sides` (`expr`, `opposite_expr`) and `symmetric` (`expr`), after `full`, mirroring extrude's M1 values. Two sides turns `expr` in the revolve's sense and `opposite_expr` against it. Symmetric turns half of `expr` each way and ignores `reverse`. The kernel's revolve takes a start and an end angle (track 1B's ADR W2-1B-2, kernel.md section 2.6).
5. **Where the records are listed.** Each value and key above goes into file-format.md section 3.9's version 3 row, written by the stage 1 integrator from this track's hand-off in the same merge (the wave 2 decisions, item 3). The generated `ptsm.schema.json` gains them with the code.
6. **Canonical forms.** Key order is declaration order: `{"kind": "up_to_face", "body": "b-2", "face": {...}, "taper": "5 deg"}`, `{"kind": "up_to_body", "target": "b-2"}`, `{"kind": "up_to_vertex", "body": "b-2", "vertex": {...}}`, `{"kind": "through_all_both"}`, `{"kind": "offset", "expr": "10 mm"}`, `{"kind": "face", "body": "b-2", "face": {...}}`, `{"kind": "two_sides", "expr": "90 deg", "opposite_expr": "45 deg"}`, `{"kind": "symmetric", "expr": "90 deg"}`. A stored `{"kind": "sketch_plane"}` start canonicalizes to no `start` key, as `"reverse": false` canonicalizes to none.
7. **Python.** Every new value and key has a dedicated form whose keyword is its field's manifest label in snake case ([ADR 0048](0048-python-keywords-gui-round-trip-and-python-budgets.md) decision 1): `end_face=`, `end_body=`, `end_vertex=`, `taper_angle=`, `start_offset=`, `start_face=`, `through_all_both_ways=True`, and for revolve `opposite_angle=` and `symmetric=True`. python-api.md gets the functions' arguments from track 2C.

## Alternatives considered

Every option is open source and adds no dependency. The first criterion of the rule picks none of them. The rule then takes the safest, then the industry standard, then the planner's recommendation. Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05).

1. *Record shape of the extents.* (a) More flat one-sided kinds, with `two_sides` staying distance-only; (b) a new tagged value whose two sides are each a one-sided extent (Fusion's model); (c) an optional `opposite` key on any one-sided kind. The rule takes (a), the safest. It is the smallest surface frozen at the gate and covers item 17 exactly. M1's records keep their shape under all three, but (a) adds the fewest keys whose meaning a later wave could need to change. Mixed two-sided extents can come later as one new tagged value (ADR 0061 decision 5).
2. *Where the taper key lives.* (a) A top-level `taper`; (b) a top-level `taper_angle`; (c) a key inside each kind that takes it. The rule takes (c), the safest. Under (a) features.md tests 8 and 9 and recompute.md test 15, which use `taper` as an unknown key, would have to change. Under (b) ui.md U-34, which uses `taper_angle` as an unknown key, would. Under (c) the schema allows a taper only where one is built. Per-side taper is Fusion's model too.
3. *How the start is stored.* (a) An optional `start` with an offset or a face; (b) an offset folded into each extent. The rule takes (a), the industry standard (Fusion's Start field). It keeps one meaning for every extent. A default value omitted when it is the value, instead of an absent key, gives the inspector a select with three choices and no special case.
4. *What a start face accepts.* (a) Any face, with the prism split at both surfaces and the middle kept; (b) flat faces parallel to the sketch plane, read as their plane. The rule takes (b), the safest. (a) needs a construction no spike measured (kernel.md section 8.5); spike 8.10 measures it, and track 4A can widen the start from its results.
5. *Through all in both directions.* (a) A new kind `through_all_both`; (b) through-all sides under option 1 (b); (c) symmetric through all. The rule takes (a), which follows decision 1.
6. *The key of the up-to-body target.* (a) `body`, as a face's body; (b) `target`. The rule takes (b), the safest: with (a) the inspector would show the face's body as a field of its own for `up_to_face` and `up_to_vertex`, where changing it would break the reference beside it.
7. *Revolve two sides in the kernel.* (a) The revolve takes start and end angles; (b) the feature rotates the profile's plane, rebuilds the profile and revolves by the total. The rule takes (a), the planner's recommendation: it mirrors extrude's start and end, rebuilds nothing and keeps the face roles. Track 1B writes the call.

## Consequences

- features/extrude.md and features/revolve.md specify the values, their validation, build, names, messages and tests. naming.md sections 3.6.2 and 3.6.3, features.md sections 3.7, 3.12 and 4.1, and model.md section 2.12 follow.
- file-format.md section 3.9's version 3 row lists every value and key of decisions 1 to 4 (stage 1 integrator). file-format.md tests 74 to 76 check the generated schema.
- ui.md's inspector rules for an optional tagged object, for selection fields inside an extent, and for the taper field come from track 2C (item 32). python-api.md's arguments and their `ValueError` sentences come from 2C (item 33).
- A two-sided or symmetric taper, a start on a face that is not flat and parallel, and mixed two-sided extents are later work. The stage 1 integrator routes them in a dated note in m2-plan.md section 5.
- ADR 0048's public Python API grows by the keywords of decision 7, on gate 19's review list.
