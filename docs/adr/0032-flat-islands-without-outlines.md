# 0032: Flat islands: no outlines, surfaces a step lighter than one flat viewport, and edges drawn only in forced colors

Status: Accepted (owner request and lead decisions, 2026-10-16), needs human review (the visual values join ui.md open issue 13). Amends the outline and surface choices of [ADR 0030](0030-islands-over-a-full-window-viewport-and-rgb-axes.md); everything else of ADR 0030 stands, and type stays as [ADR 0031](0031-self-hosted-geist-and-jetbrains-mono-for-code.md) decided.

## Context

ADR 0030 floated the chrome over a full-window viewport as islands with a `border` outline (`zinc-300` light, `zinc-700` dark) and a shadow, and kept the old hairline as `divider` inside them. The owner reviewed the island screenshots on 2026-10-16: "I don't really like how the bordered panels look. Can we drop the borders and keep the panels minimal and clean with just the faint shadow? It should look more modern, like flat design."

Without a line, the surfaces of ADR 0030 cannot separate the islands from the viewport. The light frame islands were `zinc-100` and the light viewport's gradient ended at `zinc-100` (1.00:1 at the bottom, where the timeline and status bar sit). The dark islands were darker than the viewport (`zinc-950` on `zinc-800` to `zinc-900`), so their black shadow barely showed, and ADR 0030 had raised it to 50 percent. The lead's decisions for this pass: no outline on any island, card, chip, menu, popover, tooltip, the palette, notifications, the inspector, the sketch header, the overhang legend, the view cube's Home button or the viewport controls; outlined buttons and segmented control tracks flat too; islands that still read against every point of the viewport in both themes, preferring the convention that an elevated surface is lighter than what it sits on; one faint shadow per tier; as few lines inside islands as possible; accessibility kept, forced colors included.

## Decision

Surfaces (ui.md 2.16.3, 2.16.4, 3.2):

- **Islands, cards and chips a step lighter than one flat viewport.** `surface` is `white` in the light theme and `zinc-900` in the dark theme, for every island, card and chip. `viewportTop` and `viewportBottom` become one role, `viewport`: `zinc-100` light and `zinc-950` dark, so islands measure 1.09:1 and 1.12:1 against every point of it. `stage`, the gutter, takes the same steps. The backend's background is one color (`ViewportTheme.background`).
- **What floats over the islands a step lighter again.** `surfaceRaised` is `white` in the light theme and `zinc-800` in the dark theme (1.18:1 over a dark island, 1.33:1 over the viewport), for menus, popovers, tooltips, the `expression-suggestions` list, the palette, notifications and the drop overlay, so a popup never merges with the island under it in the dark theme. The light theme has no step above white; there the stronger shadow sets a popup apart (about 1.32:1 below it and 1.09:1 beside it).
- **Translucent dark fills.** In the dark theme `hover` (and its `active` facet) is `zinc-700` at 60 percent and `tonal` `zinc-700` at 40 percent, composited over whichever surface they lie on, so one class reads on both `zinc-900` and `zinc-800` and `textMuted` keeps 4.5:1 on every fill (the lowest, 4.64, on a hovered row of a popup); `divider` is white at 10 percent in the dark theme for the same reason. A chip that is itself the surface over the viewport (the sketch header, the view cube's Home view button) takes an opaque `hover` facet, so the canvas never shows through it, and the view cube's faces carry `surface` under their translucent hover cells.
- **Chrome recedes by quiet color, size and weight, not by darkness.** The guidelines' tone line says so.

Depth (ui.md 3.2, 3.23, 2.16.6):

- **No outline.** Depth is the tonal step plus one faint shadow: `shadow-md` for what stays (frame islands and resting cards and chips, the Home view button among them) and `shadow-lg` for what comes and goes over them (overlay islands, the inspector, notifications, core panels, the palette, menus, popovers, tooltips, the dimension editor, the timeline's drop hint, the drop overlay). `elevation` is black at 10 percent light and 20 percent dark. Tailwind's shadows fall below and beside an element, never above it: in the light theme they mark an island's lower and side edges on the viewport (about 1.42:1 below and 1.20:1 beside, against the step's 1.09:1), and its top edge shows by the step alone. On the near-black dark viewport no shadow shows at any strength, so the dark step alone sets islands apart, and over the model the dark shadow darkens a body by at most about 1.6:1 just under a lower edge: soft depth, where ADR 0030's 50 percent would reach about 2.6:1, a dark halo.
- **A transparent edge for forced colors.** The `border` role becomes `forcedEdge`: `border-transparent` on the 1-pixel `border` each island, card, popup, chip, default `Button`, Search, Export and segmented track already had, on a `Switch`'s thumb, and on the rules of scrolling bodies at rest, and `outline-transparent` for the state outlines below. Nothing draws them and every box keeps its size; in forced-colors mode, where the browser drops shadows and backgrounds, the system paints a transparent border or outline in its text color, so every surface keeps a visible edge without a `forced-colors:` variant. Primary buttons, the toolbar's included, keep a border in their fill's color for the same reason.
- **State outlines.** The checked segment and every element shown selected, pressed or active by a fill (selected rows and timeline items, pressed toggles and the active tool, the palette's active option, the keyboard-active item of a menu or list) add `outline -outline-offset-1` in `forcedEdge`'s outline facet in that state, so the states a CAD user works by survive forced colors. They carry that transparent outline color at rest too, because `transition-colors` includes `outline-color` and would otherwise fade a gray or orange outline in and out with the state.
- **Content colors kept in forced colors.** The color popover's swatches and the overhang legend's ramp bar take `forced-color-adjust-none`, since forced colors would paint them in the Canvas color while the WebGL view keeps its colors; nothing else opts out.
- **Buttons.** The primary stays `accent`; every other `Button` is tonal (`tonal`, with the `hover` fill on hover); Search and Export become ghost buttons like the toolbar's. On a status banner, where the `tonal` steps measure 1.00 to 1.08 against `errorSubtle` and `warningSubtle`, a `Button` takes `variant="banner"`: `zinc-950` at 5 percent light and white at 10 percent dark (1.11:1, and 1.27 and 1.34:1), so one-click fixes keep their button shape. A pressed toggle `Button` takes `selection` in place of `tonal`.
- **Segmented controls**, as shadcn/ui's Tabs: a `tonal` track with a raised thumb (`thumb` `white`/`zinc-700`, `shadow-xs`) for the checked segment in place of the `selection` fill. The checked label's `text` at semibold names the chosen option, also in a disabled control, where it turns `textMuted`; unchecked labels turn `text` on hover (a `hover` foreground facet), since the track leaves no room for a hover fill.
- **Lines inside islands.** `divider` stays for menu separators and the rule under the palette's search field, and shows the edge of a scrolling body only while content is scrolled under a fixed header or continues under a fixed footer (`useOverflowEdges`: the side panels, the print check list, both inspectors, the palette's list). Panel header rules at rest, the palette's footer rule, the inspector footer's rule, the rules between parameter rows and above the keyboard panel's sections, the print check header's rule, the line of toolbar separators and the sketch header's inner rule go; spacing and type weight set those groups apart.

Accessibility (ui.md 2.16.6, 3.24): inputs, selects, switches, swatches and selection boxes keep their `borderControl` boundary (at least 3.08:1, on a dark popup); focus rings are unchanged (at least 3.13:1; 3.61:1 where a segment's ring falls on the dark thumb); the palette's search field stays without a boundary, as before, because its icon, placeholder and caret identify it as the open palette's one input; every text and non-text pair is measured again, and a new measured table covers the tonal steps and the model on the viewport.

Model on the new viewport (ui.md 2.16.4, 2.16.6): the default body (`zinc-400`, io.md's value) measures 2.38:1 light and 7.58:1 dark against the viewport, the light value equal to the old gradient's bottom; every body swatch's base color or chosen edge color measures at least 4.33:1, and curved outlines, which have no B-rep edge, show through shading (the White swatch 1.36 to 2.37:1 on the light viewport, the Black one 2.20 to 2.35:1 on the dark one); the dark grid moves from `zinc-600` to `zinc-700` to stay as quiet as before; every other viewport role keeps its step and its 3:1.

## Options not chosen

- **A darker light viewport** (`zinc-200`): white islands would measure 1.26:1 on every edge, top edges included. The accent's marks on the viewport (selected edges, hovered sketch items, snap targets) would fall to 2.83:1, so `highlight` would have to leave the accent for `orange-700` (4.11:1) and U-90's `highlight` equals `accent` would go; the default body would drop from 2.38 to 2.06:1, `analysisLow` from 1.21 to 1.04:1 (the overhang map's limit color would nearly vanish), the grid would need `zinc-400` (1.26 and 1.62:1), and the lowest other mark would be `axisX` at 3.56:1. `zinc-100` keeps the model's colors and `highlight` equal to the accent, at the price of top edges that show by the 1.09:1 step alone. The gate can still take this path (ui.md open issue 13).
- **Keeping a gradient:** any lighter top in the light theme brings the viewport toward the white islands (`zinc-50` gives 1.04:1 where the top bar sits), and a flat tone is the flat look the owner asked for.
- **Popups on the islands' tone in the dark theme:** a `zinc-900` menu over a `zinc-900` island measured 1.00:1 above, 1.04 beside and 1.10 below, even with a 40 percent shadow.
- **White-alpha fills in the dark theme**, as Radix's gray alpha steps: `textMuted` on white at 10 percent over a `zinc-800` popup reaches only 4.13:1, while `zinc-700` fills stay darker than `zinc-700` itself.
- **Opaque dark fills per surface** (`zinc-800` on islands, `zinc-700` in popups): `textMuted` on `zinc-700` reaches only 3.98:1, and every fill would need two class sets.
- **Dark islands on `zinc-800` over a `zinc-900` to `zinc-950` viewport:** popups would then need `zinc-700`, the same 3.98:1 problem.
- **A dark shadow at 40 or 50 percent:** it still does not show on the `zinc-950` viewport, and it darkens the model under an island by up to about 2.6:1.
- **An `outline` in place of the transparent `border`:** removing the 1-pixel border would change every island's content box (the top bar's inner height is 2.875rem of its 3rem because of it, ui.md 3.2) and the inspector's measured placement; the transparent border keeps every size.
- **`forced-colors:` variants** (`forced-colors:border`): a second set of classes on every surface for what the transparent border gives on its own.
- **A thick `border-current` thumb for the `Switch`:** border widths are CSS pixels, so `border-8` would leave a hole in a `size-4` thumb at 200 percent UI scale; the transparent 1-pixel border outlines the thumb at any scale.
- **Ghost default buttons:** the empty state's Sketch and Box and the inspector's Cancel would read as plain text without an outline; a tonal fill keeps them buttons.
- **Static rules under panel headers and above the inspector's footer:** the owner asked for fewer lines, and a rule is needed only while content runs under the header or footer.
- **A `borderControl` underline as the palette's search field boundary:** a strong line across the palette for an input its icon, placeholder and caret already identify.

## Consequences

- Every visual baseline changes (V-1 to V-21), and V-22 (forced colors), V-23 and V-24 (a menu over the islands, light and dark) and V-25 (an overlay island over the inspector, dark) are new; all need a reviewer's approval at the gate.
- U-70, U-90, U-174, U-183, U-184, U-186, U-188 and U-196 change with the roles; U-209 to U-213 and E-145 to E-147 are new.
- `roles.ts` loses `border`, moves `surface` to `white`/`zinc-900` and `surfaceRaised` to `white`/`zinc-800`, and gains `forcedEdge`, `tonal`, `thumb`, the `hover` role's `foreground`, `banner` and `opaque` facets and `divider`'s `overflowTop` and `overflowBottom` facets; `VIEWPORT_STEPS` loses `viewportTop` and `viewportBottom` for `viewport`; the backend draws a flat background.
- `Button` gains `variant="banner"`, and `src/components/overflow-edges.ts` (`useOverflowEdges`) is new.
- Two surfaces of one step still meet where only the shadow sets them apart: a submenu beside its parent menu and, under 65rem, an overlay island over the inspector (V-25).
- No dependency, no CSS file and no CSS variable is added.
