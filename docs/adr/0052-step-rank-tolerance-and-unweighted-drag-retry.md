# 0052: Gauss-Newton steps leave out rows within 1e-6 of the span, and a drag frame that fails with the drag weight is solved again without it

Status: Accepted. Amends ADR 0015's decision: the factorization of the Gauss-Newton step tests rows at `1e-6 * max(original norm, 1)`, and the diagnosis keeps the `RANK_TOLERANCE` test ADR 0015 names. Amends ADR 0004's weak pin for drag frames that fail with it, which are solved again without it. No capability, schema, license or dependency changes: two changes inside `partsmith-sketch`'s solver (sketch.md 3.7.4 and 3.9), with no public type or constant added.

## Context

- **Two property tests failed now and then at 4096 cases** (gap list SK-2): test 192 (a sketch moved by an offset solves to the moved result within 1e-6) and test 194 (a drag frame converges and its constraints hold). On 2026-10-31, 200 runs of the nightly command on the tree of 6901359 failed 8 times, 4 in each test, each on another case; the gap list had recorded 4 failures in 80 runs and 6 in 260. The 6 recorded cases and the 10 new ones, replayed from their seeds, all reproduce.
- **Test 192, a rank decision at the noise floor.** Every failing case has a constraint that only repeats others on the solution set (an `angle` that follows from a `collinear` and another `angle`; a point-line distance beside the same distance back between parallel lines; a `horizontal_distance` equal to the length of a line perpendicular to a vertical one). Its row is independent at the warm start, so the first Gauss-Newton step takes it in. After that step its part outside the span of the earlier rows is only the error left in the geometry it repeats, 1.2e-8 to 7.7e-8 in these cases, just above `RANK_TOLERANCE` (1e-8), so the next step accepts it again. The reflector built from such a part leaves rounding noise of about 1e-16 / 1e-8 in the rows after it, the size of the threshold, so whether a later row is accepted depends on the last bits of the coordinates: in the case of seed 15957013116401757264, the row after it was accepted with a part of 2.1e-8 in the original and 1.4e-8 in the translated solve, whose steps were 2.4e5 and 3.5e5 mm long. Dog-leg follows such a step to its trust radius, along a direction the constraints do not determine, so the solves slid apart by 1e-6 to 0.04 mm.
- **The same steps slide geometry in every run, not only under translation.** Of 97,574 generated cases, 7 moved a coordinate 0.39 to 6.6 mm from a warm start within 0.01 mm of the known solution, against sketch.md 3.11's minimum-norm correction. A hand-made sketch of the same kind (sketch.md test 256: a vertical line, a free collinear line, a perpendicular line with its length dimensioned twice) stretched the free line from 25 mm to 38.9 mm; of 300 random warm starts within 0.01 mm of its known solution, 239 moved a coordinate more than 0.1 mm (the step tolerance below keeps all 300 within 0.014 mm).
- **Test 194, a fold the weighted solve cannot cross.** Every failing frame drags a point out of reach where the other geometry cannot help: an arc with a fixed end and a set radius whose other end is dragged past a diameter from the fixed one (sweeps of 179.5°, 180.7° and 184.8°), or an arc's start held at a distance from a fixed point while its center is dragged away from the point. The weighted solve heads for the arc as a half circle, or for start, center and point in a line, where the arc rule and the radius (or the distance) move the center in the same direction. Only the dragged parameters can make up the rest, and the drag weight makes them 10^4 times costlier in the step's norm: dog-leg and Levenberg-Marquardt crept from residuals of 6.6e-2 to 1.2e-2 in 100 iterations each, the frame failed, and the drag stuck at its previous frame. The same frames solved without the weight converge in two iterations.
- **The properties ask for what sketch.md promises.** The generated cases have a nearby solution in every failure (a smooth solution set or a reachable lagging point), so neither generator nor property is narrower than the spec: 3.11 promises a minimum-norm correction and a result that depends only on the arguments, and 3.9 promises that a frame's constraints always hold.

## Decision

1. **The Gauss-Newton step ranks rows at 1e-6** (sketch.md 3.7.4): the step applies the sequential test of 3.8 with `1e-6 * max(original norm, 1)` in place of `RANK_TOLERANCE`, and refinement uses the same steps. A row accepted at 1e-6 or more leaves noise under about 1e-10 in the rows after it, so rounding no longer flips a decision, and a row within 1e-6 of the span is left to first-order consistency, as every dependent row is. The diagnosis keeps `RANK_TOLERANCE`, since it decides at the result. The value is a private constant of `numeric.rs`, like the trust radius floor and the step limits of 3.7.4.
2. **A drag frame that does not converge with the drag weight is solved once more from the same start with `D = I`** (sketch.md 3.9 step 5), and fails only when that fails too. A frame that converges with the weight is unchanged.

Measured with a prototype of both on 2026-10-31: every one of the 16 failing cases passes, 471 integration tests pass unchanged (test 255's literals within 1e-9), 7 of 19,569 generated solves change, by at most 4e-6, the 7 slides fall under 0.05 mm, and the nightly command passes 420 runs out of 420 (the gap list's SK-2 status line has the counts of the implementation).

## Options considered

- **Step rank test:** (a) 1e-6 for the step, `RANK_TOLERANCE` for the diagnosis (chosen); (b) `RANK_TOLERANCE` raised to 1e-6 for both: the diagnosis would also call rows within 1e-6 of the span redundant at results and change the reported `dof` and `redundant` of nearly degenerate sketches, for no failure; (c) column pivoting in the step, the most independent rows first: leaves such rows out too, but needs a second factorization beside ADR 0015's, whose plans are worked out for row order; (d) the exact trust-region step (a damped step of the Levenberg-Marquardt kind on the trust radius) instead of dog-leg's blend: also stops the slides, but changes the iteration path of every step that reaches the trust radius; (e) a narrower generator without repeating constraints: the property would stop seeing a defect users meet, since a redundant dimension is what the diagnosis is there to report.
- **Drag:** (a) a second solve without the weight (chosen); (b) more iterations: the creep is linear, a quarter of the residual in 100 iterations; (c) a continuation from the previous frame toward the cursor in smaller moves: more code and more solves per frame, and every move but the first past the reach ends at the same fold; (d) test 194 narrowed to cursors within reach: a cursor past the reach is common at the end of a drag, and the spec promises the constraints hold; (e) a weighted least-squares frame, the pin as soft rows beside the constraints as hard rows: what "as close to the cursor as they allow" means exactly, but a new drag design.

## Consequences

- sketch.md 2.1 (the comment of `RANK_TOLERANCE`), 3.7.4, 3.9 (step 5 and the paragraph after it), the new tests 256 and 257, the recorded cases of 6.13, and open issues 10, 21 and 22. Tests 192 and 194 and their generator are unchanged.
- The implementation changes `src/solver/numeric.rs` (the step's tolerance), `src/solver/mod.rs` and `src/solver/drag.rs` (the frame's second solve), commits the `cc` line of any further failing case it covers to `tests/properties.proptest-regressions` (delivery.md 2.11), and keeps the budgets of sketch.md 5: the chain drag converges with the weight, so its frames take the time they took. A frame that fails both ways, such as test 143's, now runs both methods twice before it returns the previous geometry.
- The tests commit 20 `cc` lines to `tests/properties.proptest-regressions` (delivery.md 2.11): the one the log of nightly run 40484421689 printed for its case of test 192, `cc 13ae955885c31d5c41ee7f4c2fd215b533069b8088f68ff9f407e2efa8c8ba03 # shrinks to seed = 4349971845757929081, offset_u = 0.0, offset_v = -188.8237558036373`, and the 19 cases (7 of test 192, 12 of test 194) of the 18 runs that failed in 300 runs of the nightly command on the tree of 6901359 (2026-10-31). Each fails on that tree and passes with a prototype of both decisions. The five cases recorded from local runs on 2026-10-27 have no `cc` line, and neither do the 10 new cases of the spec's runs, which no file kept; the six recorded cases are replayed from their seeds by ordinary tests (sketch.md 6.13).
- A drag past reach is not made fast: dragging the chain of sketch.md 5.2 to (600, 0), 100 mm past its reach, a frame takes about 0.96 s with step 5 (0.92 to 0.99 s when it failed before), the end lands near (439.9, 12.6), 160 mm from the cursor, and each further frame moves it a few mm closer (measured 2026-10-31 with the prototype; sketch.md open issue 22). Slides of 0.12 to 0.26 mm through a row that repeats others already at the warm start stay as they were (sketch.md open issue 21).
- The suite also runs under Node after the change (`cargo test -p partsmith-sketch --target wasm32-unknown-emscripten`, as CI's `kernel-wasm` runs it), which holds tests 256 and 257 and test 255's literals to the native values. Only CI checks Windows and macOS: the first platforms run after the change (delivery.md 2.14) is the first to compare engine results built on the new solver there.
- Solved results move only where a step met a row within 1e-6 of the span: the examples, the cross-target literals of test 255 and the web core's parity are checked again with the implementation, and an example whose result moves gets its reason in its spec and kernel.md open issue 14's perturbation check before it is blessed.
