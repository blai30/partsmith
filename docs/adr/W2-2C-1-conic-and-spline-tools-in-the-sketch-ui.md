# W2-2C-1: The conic and spline tools in the sketch UI: places, clicks, typed values and handles

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05): the M2 plan's open choice "Conic and spline tools on screen", decided as the wave 2 decisions of 2026-11-19 accept it (section 3), and the UI choices that item 14's tools need beside it. It departs from the plan's recommendation in one point, which alternative 1 records: Ellipse is a toolbar button of its own, not an entry of a group menu. It keeps the periodic toggle of [ADR 0078](0078-fit-spline-interpolation-and-spline-tool-defaults.md) (alternative 6) and changes no earlier decision. It changes no file format, protocol, naming, license, dependency or desktop capability: the tools write the records of sketch.md section 2.12 through protocol.md command 39, which [ADR 0080](0080-conic-and-spline-tools-reach-the-core-in-wave-2.md) lands. It needs no human review at the M2 gate. It resolves features/sketch.md open issue 5, and the examples of features/sketch.md test 53 keep their records.

## Context

- **The seven recipes** (sketch.md sections 2.13 and 3.21.1, ADR 0080). `ellipse`, `elliptical_arc`, `parabola`, `hyperbola`, `conic`, `control_point_spline` and `fit_point_spline` run in the core. The host sends the clicks in the recipe's fields and adds the constraints of snaps and inference itself (ADR 0080 decision 2). Previews send command 39 and then `solve_sketch` on each pointer move (ADR 0080 decision 3).
- **What the UI may compute.** The UI evaluates no conic or spline (spec README decision D15 as [ADR 0066](0066-sketch-entities-of-format-version-3-operations-and-display-polylines.md) amends it). It draws a curve of a new kind from its display polylines. Display arithmetic on values the core reports is allowed (ui.md section 1).
- **The roles** (sketch.md sections 2.4 and 3.20.1, [ADR 0081](0081-axis-roles-of-ellipses-and-hyperbolas.md)). The new kinds have the points `start`, `end`, `center`, `vertex`, `focus`, `control`, `major` and `minor`, each as its kind has it. Every point constraint and distance takes them. A drag of `.major`, `.minor` or a hyperbola's `.vertex` moves the axis it ends (sketch.md section 3.20.3). `drag_sketch` takes a point address only (protocol.md command 18).
- **The spline tool's options** (ADR 0078 decision 3). The control point spline tool writes the degree and knots of ADR 0078 and has a periodic option and no degree field. ADR 0078 alternative 6 made that option a toggle, not a close on the first pole.
- **The tool examples** (features/sketch.md tests 53 to 59). Each names the clicks of its tool. Test 53's ellipse holds a coincidence of its center with the origin, a horizontal major axis and two distances on its semi-axes. features/sketch.md open issue 5 waits for ui.md to say which values the tools take while drawing.
- **What other tools do.** Fusion's Create menu has Ellipse as an entry of its own beside Circle, Conic Curve as another, and Fit Point Spline and Control Point Spline under one Spline entry. FreeCAD's Sketcher has a conic group (ellipse by center, arc of ellipse, arc of hyperbola, arc of parabola) and a B-spline group (B-spline, periodic B-spline, B-spline by interpolation). Fusion's Ellipse takes its center, the end of one axis and a point that sets the other. Its Conic Curve takes the start, the end, then the control point, with Rho typed.
- **The cut order** (m2-plan.md section 6 item 11 and risk 11). The parabola, the hyperbola and the rho conic may move to M3.
- **Target sizes.** [ADR 0090](0090-sketch-glyphs-and-dimension-labels-get-24-pixel-targets.md) gives sketch glyphs and dimension labels 24-pixel targets, WCAG 2.2 AA's size, placed clear of the curves they mark. M1's sketch points are picked within 9.5 pixels. ui.md section 3.24 counts them among the marks of the drawing that its 24-pixel rule leaves out.

## Decision

1. **Places.** Each tool is a sketch command `sketch.<kind>`, with the recipe's `kind` as its tool id. The Sketch toolbar gains the button Ellipse after Arc and the group "Spline" (Fit point spline, Control point spline) after it. Elliptical arc, Parabola, Hyperbola and Conic are in the palette only, with Fusion's and FreeCAD's names as keywords. No tool gets a shortcut.
2. **Clicks in the recipe's order.** Each click fills the recipe's next point field, in the field order of sketch.md section 2.13, except that the conic takes its start, its end, then its control point, as Fusion's does. The last click commits. A spline tool adds a point per click and is finished by Enter or a double-click. A toggle "Closed" beside the rubber band sets `periodic`, as ADR 0078 alternative 6 decided. A click on the spline's first point adds a point like any click: the points of the shape being drawn are not in the solution and are no snap targets. Esc drops the shape being drawn, and the tool stays.
3. **Commits through the core.** The commit task runs command 39 on the sketch as the task reads it, takes the entities the recipe added, adds the constraints of the snaps and inferences that name them, and commits with M1's probe (ui.md section 3.16.3). Its label is the tool's title. A refused commit keeps the shape for another try: the five tools that commit on a click keep their clicks but the last, and a spline keeps all its points.
4. **Previews.** On each pointer move the scaffold is drawn at once from the clicks (points, the axis lines, the conic's control lines, the control polygon), and the curve when the solve answers, by ADR 0080 decision 3. A step whose recipe lacks a click sends a stand-in recipe built from the clicks and the cursor by display arithmetic (a mirror image across an axis line), or nothing. A recipe that refuses its input shows the core's message as the sketch hint, and a click at that step is ignored.
5. **Typed values.** The ellipse and the elliptical arc take Major radius and Minor radius, the hyperbola Major radius, and the parabola Focal length. Each is a length that locks its point and adds a driving `distance` between the two points it spans. The conic takes Rho, a plain number from 0 to 1 that starts at 0.5 and is stored in the record, never as a constraint. The splines take none.
6. **Inference.** A snap at a click applies to the role the click places. The major axis of an ellipse, an elliptical arc or a hyperbola, and the axis of a parabola, infer `horizontal` or `vertical` in the points form, as a line does. A typed length then runs along the inferred direction. No tangent is inferred for the new kinds in M2.
7. **Handles.** The UI draws and picks the role points from the solution's `points`, which name every role of every entity with its solved position, and the curve's ends from the same list. A role point is a handle and a 24-pixel target: the pointer picks it within 12 CSS pixels (times the UI scale), and a drag sends it to `drag_sketch`. A spline is shaped by dragging its point entities. A curve of a new kind is not dragged whole in M2, as M1 drags no curve.
8. **Glyphs.** A constraint glyph on a curve of a new kind sits at the middle of its display polyline by length, on the outside of the bend, with ADR 0090's offsets and steps.

## Alternatives considered

Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05): the most open-source approach, then the safest, then the industry standard, then this work's recommendation. Every option is in-house code under Apache-2.0. The first criterion ties.

1. *Where the tools sit.* (i) Every tool in the toolbar; (ii) a group menu for the conics and one for the splines, as FreeCAD has; (iii) the plan's recommendation: Ellipse and the two splines in toolbar group menus, the other four in the palette; (iv) the palette only. The options are as safe as each other. Fusion and FreeCAD disagree on the conics, and no one standard decides. The rule takes (iii), the recommendation. It follows the M2 plan's principle 5 (fewer options on screen), and the cut candidates stay off the toolbar: a cut moves no button. Within (iii), Ellipse could be (a) a button of its own or (b) an entry of a group menu. With the other conics in the palette, the only group it could join is M1's Circle turned into a group. The two are as safe as each other. Fusion's Create menu lists Ellipse as an entry of its own beside Circle, and the industry standard takes (a). This is the one departure from the recommendation's wording, which put Ellipse in a group menu.
2. *Shortcuts.* (i) None; (ii) letters such as E for Ellipse and S for Spline. E is Extrude in every context (ui.md section 3.5), and S opens the palette. The rule takes (i), the safest: no shortcut changes meaning.
3. *The conic's clicks.* (i) The recipe's field order: start, control, end; (ii) Fusion's order: start, end, control. The two are as safe as each other. The rule takes (ii), the industry standard: the chord is set first, and the control point shapes the curve under the cursor.
4. *Typed values.* (i) None; (ii) the semi-axes, the focal length and Rho as above; (iii) every quantity, the angles of the arcs' ends included. (iii) needs the curve's parameter at a typed angle, which is curve evaluation in the UI. (i) leaves test 53's distances to be added by hand. The rule takes (ii), the safest that resolves features/sketch.md open issue 5. Each value is a distance between two role points that M1's `distance` already takes, and no new constraint type is needed. Rho is a field of the record.
5. *Where role points come from.* (i) The solution's `points`; (ii) arithmetic on the record's fields (`center + major`, `center + minor_radius * n`) and the ends of the polylines; (iii) evaluation of the curves in the UI. (iii) breaks D15. (ii) holds D15 but computes in TypeScript what the core already computes. The rule takes (i), the safest: the positions are the solver's, and the list carries them for M1's roles already (engine.md section 3.6's projection, which item 14 extends to the new roles).
6. *Dragging a whole curve.* (i) Points only, as M1; (ii) a drag of the curve through a new `drag_sketch` target. (ii) changes the protocol. The rule takes (i), the safest. ui.md open issue 7 keeps curve drags for later.
7. *The size of a role handle.* (i) M1's 9.5 pixels, as a mark of the drawing that ui.md section 3.24 leaves out; (ii) 12 pixels, a 24-pixel target as ADR 0090 gives glyphs and labels. The rule takes (ii), the safest: it meets WCAG 2.2 AA's target size without an exception, and the glyph at a point still clears it (ADR 0090's point offset puts the glyph's nearest corner about 13.03 pixels away). WCAG 2.2 AA is also the industry standard ADR 0090 took. M1's points keep 9.5 pixels: changing them changes M1's tests, and no new handle needs it.

- features/sketch.md (track 2A, stage 2): test 53 names the typed values of its clicks, and open issue 5 is resolved. Test 53's example keeps its records: the typed values add the two distances it already holds, the snap adds its coincidence and the inference its horizontal.
