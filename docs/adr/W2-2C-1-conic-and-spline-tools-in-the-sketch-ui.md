# W2-2C-1: The conic and spline tools in the sketch UI: places, clicks, typed values and handles

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05): the M2 plan's open choice "Conic and spline tools on screen", decided as the wave 2 decisions of 2026-11-19 accept it (section 3), and the UI choices that item 14's tools need beside it. It changes no file format, protocol, naming, license, dependency or desktop capability: the tools write the records of sketch.md section 2.12 through protocol.md command 39, which [ADR 0080](0080-conic-and-spline-tools-reach-the-core-in-wave-2.md) lands. It needs no human review at the M2 gate. It resolves features/sketch.md open issue 5, and the examples of features/sketch.md test 53 keep their records.

## Context

- **The seven recipes** (sketch.md sections 2.13 and 3.21.1, ADR 0080). `ellipse`, `elliptical_arc`, `parabola`, `hyperbola`, `conic`, `control_point_spline` and `fit_point_spline` run in the core. The host sends the clicks in the recipe's fields and adds the constraints of snaps and inference itself (ADR 0080 decision 2). Previews send command 39 and then `solve_sketch` on each pointer move (ADR 0080 decision 3).
- **What the UI may compute.** The UI evaluates no conic or spline (spec README decision D15 as [ADR 0066](0066-sketch-entities-of-format-version-3-operations-and-display-polylines.md) amends it). It draws a curve of a new kind from its display polylines. Display arithmetic on values the core reports is allowed (ui.md section 1).
- **The roles** (sketch.md sections 2.4 and 3.20.1, [ADR 0081](0081-axis-roles-of-ellipses-and-hyperbolas.md)). The new kinds have the points `start`, `end`, `center`, `vertex`, `focus`, `control`, `major` and `minor`, each as its kind has it. Every point constraint and distance takes them. A drag of `.major`, `.minor` or a hyperbola's `.vertex` moves the axis it ends (sketch.md section 3.20.3). `drag_sketch` takes a point address only (protocol.md command 18).
- **The tool examples** (features/sketch.md tests 53 to 59). Each names the clicks of its tool. Test 53's ellipse holds a coincidence of its center with the origin, a horizontal major axis and two distances on its semi-axes. features/sketch.md open issue 5 waits for ui.md to say which values the tools take while drawing.
- **What other tools do.** Fusion has Ellipse, Conic Curve, Fit Point Spline and Control Point Spline in its Create menu, with the two splines under one Spline entry. FreeCAD's Sketcher has a conic group (ellipse by center, arc of ellipse, arc of hyperbola, arc of parabola) and a B-spline group (B-spline, periodic B-spline, B-spline by interpolation). Fusion's Ellipse takes its center, the end of one axis and a point that sets the other. Its Conic Curve takes the start, the end, then the control point, with Rho typed. Both apps close a spline when the last click lands on the first point.
- **The cut order** (m2-plan.md section 6 item 11 and risk 11). The parabola, the hyperbola and the rho conic may move to M3.
- **ADR 0090** gives sketch glyphs 24-pixel targets placed clear of the curves they mark, with offsets that follow the curve's direction on screen.

## Decision

1. **Places.** Each tool is a sketch command `sketch.<kind>`, with the recipe's `kind` as its tool id. The Sketch toolbar gains the button Ellipse after Arc and the group "Spline" (Fit point spline, Control point spline) after it. Elliptical arc, Parabola, Hyperbola and Conic are in the palette only, with Fusion's and FreeCAD's names as keywords. No tool gets a shortcut.
2. **Clicks in the recipe's order.** Each click fills the recipe's next point field, in the field order of sketch.md section 2.13, except that the conic takes its start, its end, then its control point, as Fusion's does. The last click commits. A spline tool adds a point per click and is finished by Enter, a double-click, or a click on its first point, which makes it closed. A toggle "Closed" sets `periodic` too. Esc drops the shape being drawn, and the tool stays.
3. **Commits through the core.** The commit task runs command 39 on the sketch as the task reads it, takes the entities the recipe added, adds the constraints of the snaps and inferences that name them, and commits with M1's probe (ui.md section 3.16.3). Its label is the tool's title.
4. **Previews.** On each pointer move the scaffold is drawn at once from the clicks (points, the axis lines, the conic's control lines, the control polygon), and the curve when the solve answers, by ADR 0080 decision 3. A recipe that refuses its input shows the core's message as the sketch hint, and the click that would commit is ignored.
5. **Typed values.** The ellipse and the elliptical arc take Major radius and Minor radius, the hyperbola Major radius, and the parabola Focal length, each a length that locks its point and adds a driving `distance` between the two points it spans. The conic takes Rho, a number from 0 to 1 that starts at 0.5 and is stored in the record, never as a constraint. The splines take none.
6. **Inference.** A snap at a click applies to the role the click places. The major axis of an ellipse, an elliptical arc or a hyperbola, and the axis of a parabola, infer `horizontal` or `vertical` in the points form, as a line does. No tangent is inferred for the new kinds in M2.
7. **Handles.** The UI draws and picks the role points from the solution's `points`, which name every role of every entity with its solved position, and the curve's ends from the same list. A role point is a handle: a drag sends it to `drag_sketch`. A spline is shaped by dragging its point entities. A curve of a new kind is not dragged whole in M2, as M1 drags no curve.
8. **Glyphs.** A constraint glyph on a curve of a new kind sits at the middle of its display polyline by length, on the outside of the bend, with ADR 0090's offsets and steps.

## Alternatives considered

Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05): the most open-source approach, then the safest, then the industry standard, then this work's recommendation. Every option is in-house code under Apache-2.0, so the first criterion ties.

1. *Where the tools sit.* (i) Every tool in the toolbar; (ii) a group menu for the conics and one for the splines, as FreeCAD has; (iii) Ellipse and the splines in the toolbar, the others in the palette; (iv) the palette only. The options are as safe as each other. Fusion puts Ellipse, Conic Curve and Spline in its Create menu, FreeCAD all of them in groups, so no one standard decides. The rule takes (iii), the planner's recommendation: the M2 plan's principle 5 asks for fewer options on screen, and the cut candidates stay off the toolbar, so a cut moves no button. The recommendation put Ellipse in a group menu. No group of M1's toolbar holds it, and turning Circle into a group would add a click to every circle, so Ellipse is a button of its own.
2. *Shortcuts.* (i) None; (ii) letters such as E for Ellipse and S for Spline. E is Extrude in every context (ui.md section 3.5) and S opens the palette. The rule takes (i), the safest: no shortcut changes meaning.
3. *The conic's clicks.* (i) The recipe's field order: start, control, end; (ii) Fusion's order: start, end, control. The two are as safe as each other. The rule takes (ii), the industry standard: the chord is set first and the control point shapes the curve under the cursor.
4. *Closing a spline.* (i) A toggle only; (ii) a click on the first point only; (iii) both. ADR 0078 decision 3 gives the tool a periodic option. The rule takes (iii): the toggle is the option ADR 0078 names, and keyboard users reach it, while the click on the first point is how Fusion and FreeCAD users close a spline. A toggle alone would leave that click as one more point at the same place, which the recipe refuses (`RepeatedPoint`).
5. *Typed values.* (i) None; (ii) the semi-axes, the focal length and Rho as above; (iii) every quantity, the angles of the arcs' ends included. (iii) needs the curve's parameter at a typed angle, which is curve evaluation in the UI. (i) leaves test 53's distances to be added by hand. The rule takes (ii), the safest that resolves features/sketch.md open issue 5: each value is a distance between two role points that M1's `distance` already takes, so no new constraint type is needed, and Rho is a field of the record.
6. *Where role points come from.* (i) The solution's `points`; (ii) arithmetic on the record's fields (`center + major`, `center + minor_radius * n`) and the ends of the polylines; (iii) evaluation of the curves in the UI. (iii) breaks D15. (ii) holds D15 but computes in TypeScript what the core already computes. The rule takes (i), the safest: the positions are the solver's, and the list carries them for M1's roles already (engine.md section 3.6's projection, which item 14 extends to the new roles).
7. *Dragging a whole curve.* (i) Points only, as M1; (ii) a drag of the curve through a new `drag_sketch` target. (ii) changes the protocol. The rule takes (i), the safest. ui.md open issue 7 keeps curve drags for later.
