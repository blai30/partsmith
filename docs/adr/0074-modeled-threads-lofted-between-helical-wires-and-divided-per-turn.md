# 0074: Modeled threads lofted between helical wires and divided per turn

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05); needs human review at the M2 gate: it accepts M8 x 1.25 x 20's measured time against kernel.md section 8.2's 300 ms, fixed before the spike ran (decision 3). It settles the M2 plan's choice 15 with the result of the thread spike (kernel.md section 8.2), as the plan and [ADR 0071](0071-kernel-spikes-and-facade-calls-that-poll-an-interrupt.md) decision 3 ask. Neither construction passed every input. Section 8.2's Decision then asks for an ADR that lists the failing sizes. This ADR keeps option (a), with one edge per turn and the tooth's faces divided at every turn, and accepts the measured time of M8 x 1.25 x 20. It changes no file format, protocol schema, naming algorithm, license or dependency. `ShapeUpgrade_ShapeDivideAngle` is in `TKShHealing`, already linked (kernel.md section 3.12 item 3).

## Context

- **Choice 15.** Option (a) makes each helix a line on the parameter plane of a cylinder or a cone, exact through `BRepLib`, and the thread's faces by `BRepOffsetAPI_ThruSections` between helical wires, the construction of OCCT's bottle tutorial. Option (b) sweeps the thread profile along an approximated helix with `BRepOffsetAPI_MakePipeShell`. The plan picked (a), "confirmed or replaced by wave 1's spike on a corpus of thread sizes, with the result in kernel.md and an ADR".
- **Section 8.2's Pass.** (a) passes when every input is valid as built, both later booleans (a slot cut, then a box fuse) are valid, 20 samples of each crest and root edge lie within 1e-6 mm of the exact helix, parity holds, and M8 x 1.25 x 20 with its boolean builds within 300 ms natively. (b) passes on the same terms with 1e-4 mm. Section 8.1 item 5 adds a size delta of at most 1 MiB and, for the cap's thread, a longest poll gap of at most 150 ms. When neither passes every input, an ADR lists the failing sizes, and features/thread.md offers a cosmetic thread in their failure message.
- **(a) as written.** One `ThruSections` between closed wires of one edge per helix. Its caps lie on the periodic cylinder or cone over many periods (u over 92.7 rad for M8). The fuse that joins the tooth to its base works. A later boolean classifies points against such a face in its first period and picks the wrong turn. 16 slot cuts returned a valid empty shape, and the box fuse refused it. It passes 2 of the 19 inputs, the cap and the neck. With one edge per turn it passes 3.
- **(a) divided per turn.** One edge per turn, then `ShapeUpgrade_ShapeDivideAngle(2 pi)` on the tooth with its frame turned to the thread's start. Each face then spans one period of its surface. It passes every per-input criterion on all 19 inputs: valid as built, both later booleans valid as built, every crest and root sample within 7.5e-8 mm, parity within 1.1e-14 relative. The spike's whole branch adds 707,153 bytes, and the cap's longest poll gap is 4.443 ms. M8 x 1.25 x 20 takes 416.5 ms on the rod and 418.0 ms in the block natively, the medians of three sets. Every set is over 300 ms (350.8 to 434.2 ms).
- **(b).** As written, its inner side lies within 1e-4 mm of the base's cylinder but not on it. The boolean of these near-coincident faces returned valid shapes that lost the base, invalid shapes, or `Standard_OutOfMemory` at 6 GB on five blocks. Under Node the module aborted at 4 GB. It passes no input with parity: it holds parity only on the M30 x 2 rod, whose result lost the base. With the profile run 0.05 mm into the base, it differs across targets on all 18 inputs (1.8e-9 to 6.2e-4 relative) and misses 1e-4 mm on three rods.
- **Time grows with the turns.** (a) divided per turn takes 128.0 ms on the coil (5 turns), 160.0 ms on the cap (4.75 turns) and 1,031 ms on the M3 x 0.5 x 20 rod (38.75 turns). Section 8.2 sets gate 10's row for the thread in wave 4 from its measured median.

## Decision

1. **Construction (a), divided per turn.** features/thread.md (wave 2) specifies the thread as the spike's `lofted-divided`. Each helix is a `Geom2d_Line` on the parameter plane of a `Geom_CylindricalSurface` or `Geom_ConicalSurface`, cut into one edge per turn. Each edge's 3D curve comes from `BRepLib::BuildCurve3d` within 1e-7 mm, with its segments given and the result checked. A ruled, solid `BRepOffsetAPI_ThruSections` joins the inner and outer wires. `ShapeUpgrade_ShapeDivideAngle(2 pi)` divides the tooth with its frame turned to the thread's start. The fuse or cut of kernel.md section 3.4.5 joins it to the base. It counts as choice 15's (a): its faces are `ThruSections`' faces between exact helical wires, divided so each spans one period of its surface.
2. **The failing sizes**, as section 8.2's Decision asks:
   - (a) divided per turn: M8 x 1.25 x 20, by the 300 ms alone (416.5 ms on the rod, 418.0 ms in the block). No size fails a per-input criterion.
   - (a) as written: every size but M30 x 2 at the cap's 12 mm (the cap and the neck), and M8 x 1.25 x 20 also by the time.
   - (a) with one edge per turn, undivided: every size but M30 x 2 at the cap's 12 mm (M3 x 0.5 x 20 fails in the block only), and M8 x 1.25 x 20 also by the time (338.3 ms on the rod, 413.1 ms in the block).
   - (b) with the overlap: every size by parity. M3, M5 and 1/4-20 also by exactness, and M8 x 1.25 x 20 also by the time.
   - (b) as written: every size.
3. **The measured time is accepted.** M8 x 1.25 x 20 is modeled like every other size. This sets aside, for the product, the 300 ms that section 8.2's Pass fixed before the run. The verdict stays as measured: (a) divided per turn fails the Pass on that size, and item 2 lists it. ADR 0071's alternative 2 keeps a criterion from bending to a result, and this verdict did not bend. Section 8.2's Decision gives the case where neither construction passes to this ADR, and this decision only settles what the product builds in that case. Gate 10's row for the thread is set in wave 4 from its measured median plus 20 percent ([ADR 0057](0057-budgets-set-from-the-measured-median-plus-20-percent.md)), the median of three sets.
4. **The cosmetic thread in the failure message.** The message of a modeled thread that does not build offers the cosmetic thread (m2-plan.md risk 1). With item 1's construction, no input of the spike needs it.

## Alternatives considered

Every option is open source, built on OCCT. The first criterion of the rule picks none of them. The rule then takes the safest, then the industry standard, then the recommendation of this work. Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05).

1. *The construction.* Options: (i) (a) with one edge per turn and its faces divided per turn; (ii) (a) as section 8.2 writes it; (iii) (a) with one edge per turn, undivided; (iv) (b) as written; (v) (b) with the profile run 0.05 mm into the base. The rule takes (i), the safest. It is the only construction whose every input is valid, with both later booleans valid and equal results on both targets. (ii) and (iii) return valid empty shapes from later booleans, a failure `Shape::is_valid()` does not see. (iv) can exhaust memory, which under Node ends the web core's worker. (v) differs across targets on every input. (i) keeps the plan's reasons for (a): an exact helix and lofted faces. The division is OCCT's own healing algorithm for faces on periodic surfaces.
2. *The time of M8 x 1.25 x 20.* Options: (i) accept the measured time, with gate 10's row set from its median; (ii) model no thread of that size, and offer the cosmetic thread in its message; (iii) hold features/thread.md until a construction builds it within 300 ms. The rule takes (i), the safest: the thread builds valid, exact and equal on both targets. Under (ii) a valid thread is refused, and every size with more turns falls under the same bar (M3 x 0.5 x 20 takes 1,031 ms on a rod). (iii) leaves the choice waiting, which the owner's rule excludes. (i) is also this repository's practice: ADR 0057 sets every budget from its measured median plus 20 percent.

## Consequences

- features/thread.md (wave 2, item 20) specifies item 1's construction, its history and its tests from kernel.md section 8.2's Results.
- History: the crest and root helix edges are `ThruSections`' section edges (kernel.md section 8.2). `ThruSections::Generated` misnames a flank line on a wire walked reversed (kernel.md open issue 22). features/thread.md therefore builds its section wires with every edge forward, or names the flank lines by their two faces (kernel.md open issue 21 (a)).
  - Note (2026-11-23): by [ADR 0084](0084-wrap-curves-and-modeled-threads-in-the-kernel.md) (Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05)), the kernel's `modeled_thread`, not features/thread.md, builds the section wires, each with every edge forward (kernel.md section 3.4.14 item 5.4). This settles kernel.md open issue 21 (a) for the kernel. features/thread.md still chooses whether the flank lines are named by these generators or by their two faces (naming.md section 3.6).
- Crest and root faces split per turn, and their face-rule names are order dependent (m2-plan.md risk 3). The roles `crest` and `root` sit on edges, which the builder names.
- features/thread.md's tests check that a thread's boolean kept both arguments, which `Shape::is_valid()` does not see (kernel.md open issue 22). Item 1's division removes the faces on which `Shape::volume` misreads.
- The thread's time grows with its turns (kernel.md section 8.2). Its budget needs the median of three sets.
- The cap's thread with its boolean meets section 8.1 item 5's poll gap with room for gate 11's cancel of the cap's preview (kernel.md section 8.2).
- The spike's whole branch, with both constructions and every variant, sets the size delta (kernel.md section 8.2, delivery.md section 3.5). Item 1's construction was not built alone.
- kernel.md section 8.2's Results and ADR 0071's note of 2026-11-16 point here.
