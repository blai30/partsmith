# 0041: Thin walls are measured only between faces that face each other, so no diagonal at an acute edge decides a finding

Status: Accepted, except the value of `WALL_FACING_ANGLE` (30 degrees), which needs human review with print.md section 8's thresholds; the four blessed expected files and the new `shell/thin-wall` example are reviewed in the pull request (delivery.md 2.8).

## Context

- **The second platforms run.** Run 40478176115 of `platforms.yml` (dispatched on `main`, 2026-10-27, commit 5153d61, with the fixes of the first run, ADR 0039 among them) passed the crate tests and clippy on `windows-2025` and `macos-15`, kernel.md tests 137 and 138 among them, so the shim's triangulator gives the same triangles for the same nodes on both. The examples runner failed on both, and only under `/checks/bodies/0/report/checks/5` (`thin_walls`) of `cone/pointed`, `cone/apex-below-tolerance`, `cone/frustum` and `cone/inverted`, and the summary line that counts them (delivery.md open issue 1 (d)):
  - `macos-15` (job 122101627953): `cone/pointed` and `cone/apex-below-tolerance` 5 warnings instead of 4 (`checks/5/findings/2` with other bounds, `findings/4` unexpected); `cone/frustum` regions 2 and 3 with other bounds and values (0.012548768981348442 against 0.012546811460426037, 0.012542323133553994 against 0.012548768981348454); `cone/inverted` 9 warnings instead of 8 (`findings/0`, `/1` and `/3` other, `findings/6` unexpected).
  - `windows-2025` (job 122101627964): one line, `cone/inverted`'s `checks/5/findings/1/value` 0.006294717528121916 against 0.006299536308159865.
- **What those findings measured.** The flagged regions lie along the rim where a cone's flat end meets its sloped side, at 45° on `cone/pointed` and `cone/apex-below-tolerance` (radius 10, height 10) and at 63.4° on `cone/frustum` (radii 10 and 5) and `cone/inverted` (radius 5 at the top). A flat end is triangulated from its 144 rim points only (2.5° steps, all on one circle), so the triangles next to the rim are ears and slivers, and a ray from such a triangle's centroid hits the sloped side at a distance proportional to how far the centroid lies from the rim: an ear of three neighboring rim points has its centroid two thirds of the depth of its 5° chord inside the circle (0.0063 mm at radius 10), which the 45° rim turns into the 0.0063 mm walls of the pointed cones and the 63.4° rims into the 0.0125 mm and 0.0063 mm walls of the frustum and the inverted cone. These are not walls. Every triangulation of cocircular points is a Delaunay triangulation, so the rim points' last bits pick the diagonals, and with them the centroids.
- **Windows, reproduced.** MSVC compiles OCCT with `/fp:precise` (OCCT's own CMake flags; `scripts/build-occt.sh` adds none), which fuses nothing on x64, so the C runtime's math is the source there. On Linux, a wrapper preloaded around the examples runner that raises only glibc's cosine of `0x1.4984a650d3328p+1` (the 147.5° rim node of `cone/inverted`), in `cos` and in `sincos`, by one ulp changes exactly one value of the corpus, `cone/inverted`'s `checks/5/findings/1/value`, from 0.006299536308159865 to 0.006294717528121916: the Windows line bit for bit.
- **macOS, not attributed.** On arm64 both Apple's libm and fused operations in OCCT's own arithmetic move nodes in their last bits (a rim point is `R cos u` and `R sin u` in an axis-aligned frame, exact even where operations are fused, but its parameter `u` comes from OCCT's discretization of the circle), and this run cannot tell which moved the rim points. The decision below does not depend on it.
- **How sensitive the old rule was.** The same wrapper, moving one result in 4 of `sin`, `cos` and `sincos` up or down (four seeds), one in 32 (two seeds), or every one of them up, changed the thin walls of the cones in all seven of those modes (13 to 81 lines, all under `thin_walls` and the summary; all four cones in six modes, `cone/frustum` alone in one), with the expected files of b2ca9b5.
- **Kernel.md open issue 14** named two answers once test 137 passed with a mesh-dependent result left: OCCT built with `-ffp-contract=off` on macOS, or print checks that do not depend on the diagonals of a degenerate triangulation. Windows fuses nothing and differed too, so the first cannot be the answer.

## Decision

1. **A thickness ray measures a wall only between faces that face each other** (print.md 3.9.6 item 2): the hit distance counts when the hit is not a front face and the hit triangle has a normal `n_h` with `n · n_h <= -cos(WALL_FACING_ANGLE + ANGLE_TOLERANCE)`, where `n` is the normal of the ray's own triangle and `WALL_FACING_ANGLE` is π/6 (print.md 2.1). A hit triangle without a normal gives no value, as a triangle without one does. `faces_each_other` in `crates/partsmith-print/src/checks/thin_walls.rs` is the rule.
2. **Why 30 degrees.** A rib drafted up to about 15° per side stays a wall. A curved wall's facets stay within about the tessellation's angular deviation of opposite (5°, 10° at `draft`, print.md 3.4 and engine.md), far inside the angle. The rims of cones and wedges of 45° and more do not count: for these cones every hit a ray can make is 45° or more from opposite (the pointed cones: disc to side 45°, side to side 90°; the frustum and the inverted cone: disc to side 63.4°, side to side 53.1°, side to the other disc 116.6°), except disc to disc on the frustum, which measures its 10 mm height, and faceting moves a side triangle's normal by about the angular deviation, far from the 15° margin. So their results are the same under any triangulation of the ends, from any libm and any fused arithmetic. `ANGLE_TOLERANCE` puts faces exactly 30° apart (print.md test 115's WEDGE_30) on the wall side on every platform: it moves the threshold 5e-5 in the dot product away from the exact angle, far beyond the last bits of a normal or of the platform's cosine of the threshold.
3. **No kernel change.** ADR 0039, kernel.md test 137 and its triangle list stay.
4. **The four cone files are blessed again on Linux** (table below), since the spec changed their results; nothing in them but the thin walls, the body's health and the summary changes. `shell/thin-wall` (`shell/open-top` with a 0.6 mm wall) is added, so the platforms keep comparing a real thin wall: its faces are exactly opposite and axis-aligned, so the rule keeps both its findings and no diagonal decides them.
5. **Kernel.md open issue 14 records option (b) as the next answer** if a triangulation-dependent result still shows on a platform.

## Options considered

- (a) **The facing rule** (chosen).
- (b) **Rounding Delabella's input in the shim** to multiples of 2^-24 (`std::nearbyint(value * 0x1p24) / 0x1p24` on the unit-square coordinates of `DelabellaBaseAlgorithm::buildBaseTriangulation`, OCCT's positions kept), so nodes that differ only in their last bits give the same triangles. The Windows diagnosis's prototype (not measured again here) reported the four cones' reports identical under every `sin`, `cos` and `sincos` result moved one ulp up, down, alternating, and rounded from `long double`; all 183 examples kept their exit codes and only the cones changed; `partsmith-occt-sys` integration 423 of 424 (test 137's list changes). Not taken now: it keeps the artifact findings (`cone/pointed` and `cone/apex-below-tolerance` 10 warnings, `cone/inverted` 9, the frustum none), amends ADR 0039 decision 4, leaves OCCT's node insertion and Watson and any coordinate within its platform difference of a rounding boundary to chance, and adds a grid constant whose collapse of nodes closer than 2^-24 of a face's box no test covers. Kept as the next answer.
- (c) **OCCT built with `-ffp-contract=off` on macOS.** Windows fuses nothing and still differed, no flag removes a libm difference, and every OCCT cache would build again.
- (d) **A wider tolerance or per-platform expected files.** Forbidden (delivery.md 2.14, ADR 0038), and a tolerance cannot absorb a count.
- (e) **Both (a) and (b).** (b) adds kernel risk for no difference left in the corpus.
