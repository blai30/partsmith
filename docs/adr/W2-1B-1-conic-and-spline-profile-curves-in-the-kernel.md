# W2-1B-1: Conic and spline profile curves in the kernel

Status: Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05). It specifies the kernel half of the M2 plan's item 14 in kernel.md sections 2.5, 3.4.2 and 3.4.4: the `PlanarCurve` variants of the sketch's conics and splines, how a piece of a split spline reaches the kernel, how the kernel checks and measures them, and the revolve's half-plane check on them. It changes no file format, protocol schema, naming algorithm, license or dependency, and needs no new OCCT toolkit: `Geom2d` is in `TKG2d`, and `GeomLib`, `Extrema`, `GCPnts` and `Geom2dConvert` are in `TKGeomBase`, both already linked (kernel.md section 3.12 item 3). It needs no human review at the M2 gate. Its public API changes (`PlanarCurve` and `TaggedCurve` lose `Copy`, five variants and `Vector2`) reach only `partsmith-features`.

## Context

- **What the plan fixed.** m2-plan.md choice 11 builds the sketch's curves as exact OCCT curves, with conics and fit splines as rational or polynomial B-splines. kernel.md section 2.5 (wave 1) named the variants `Ellipse`, `EllipticalArc`, `Parabola`, `Hyperbola` and `Bspline`, one per `ProfileCurve` variant of sketch.md section 3.20.10, with the same names, and left their fields, checks, messages, signed areas and tests to item 14's kernel spec.
- **Pieces.** The sketch's arrangement splits curves at their intersections (sketch.md section 3.13.2). A piece of an arc keeps its circle and takes the vertices as its ends. A piece of a B-spline cannot be described by its end points alone: on a closed or self-crossing spline a point can lie at several parameters. kernel.md section 2.5 as written had no field for a piece. The decisions of 2026-11-19 (wave 2's spec round, item 2) take option (a) of the plan's choice "How a piece of a split conic or spline is represented": the whole curve plus a parameter range, built in the shim as a `Geom2d_TrimmedCurve` and checked against its neighbors within `LINEAR`. Track 1A writes the matching `ProfileCurve` fields in sketch.md section 2.7 and the ADR of the sketch side.
- **What OCCT 7.9.3 requires** (read in its sources on 2026-11-19). `gp_Elips2d` refuses a minor radius larger than the major radius, which sketch.md section 3.20.1 allows. `gp_Hypr2d` takes any two radii. `Geom2d_BSplineCurve` refuses a degree above 25 (`BSplCLib::MaxDegree()`), two different knots within `Epsilon` of the smaller one (the distance to the next double) and a weight of at most `gp::Resolution()` (`DBL_MIN`). Its periodic form takes `poles + 1` knots of multiplicity 1, and its span from knot `j` combines the `degree + 1` poles from pole `j` on, cyclically. `BRepLib::BuildCurve3d` of an edge on a `Geom_Plane` is `GeomLib::To3d`, exact for lines, conics, B-splines and trimmed curves. `BRepBuilderAPI_MakeEdge2d` builds on `BRepLib::Plane()`, a `Geom_Plane` that OCCT creates on its first call and keeps for the process.
- **What the facade does today.** `make_profile` checks, chains and orients every loop in Rust (`src/profiles.rs`): it takes the ends of lines and arcs from their fields and sums the signed area of lines, arcs and circles in closed form. The revolve's half-plane check (`src/sweeps.rs`) takes exact extremes of lines, arcs and circles. This crate depends only on `partsmith-mesh`, so it cannot call the sketch's in-house spline evaluation (m2-plan.md choice 10).

## Decision

1. **Variants with the sketch's geometry.** `PlanarCurve` gains `Ellipse { center, major, minor_radius }`, `EllipticalArc { center, major, minor_radius, start, end }`, `Parabola { vertex, focus, start, end }`, `Hyperbola { center, major, minor_radius, start, end }` and `Bspline { degree, poles, weights, knots, multiplicities, periodic, range }`, with the value type `Vector2` for `major`. They carry the geometry of sketch.md section 2.7's `ProfileCurve` variants without the direction flags (`ccw`, `forward`), as `Arc` does. A `Bspline` leaves out the sketch's `start` and `end`, which the kernel measures, and takes its knots in OCCT's form: the sketch's runs of equal knot values become distinct knots with multiplicities (features.md section 3.7 converts them by kernel.md section 2.5 item 1's rule). `PlanarCurve` and `TaggedCurve` derive `Clone` and no longer `Copy`.
2. **A piece is the whole curve plus its range.** A piece of a spline or conic that the sketch split is the whole `Bspline` with `range`, its parameter interval `[low, high]` in the spline's own parameter (on a periodic spline it may run past the last knot). The shim builds it as a `Geom2d_TrimmedCurve` and measures its points at `low` and `high`, and the chain checks them against the neighbors within `LINEAR`. A piece of an ellipse, a parabola or a hyperbola is an `EllipticalArc`, `Parabola` or `Hyperbola` with the piece's ends. Their parameters are those of the conic's nearest points, which are unique, so they need no range.
3. **Exact curves on the profile's own plane.** The shim builds each curve as an exact `Geom2d` curve in plane coordinates and its edge with `BRepBuilderAPI_MakeEdge` on the profile's `Geom_Plane`, with `BRepLib::BuildCurve3d` for the 3D curve. It never uses `BRepBuilderAPI_MakeEdge2d` or `BRepLib::Plane()`. An ellipse whose minor radius is the larger is built with its axes swapped, the same points in the same direction. A hyperbola never is.
4. **Knots in OCCT's form.** `Bspline` carries distinct knots and multiplicities, checked by OCCT's own rules (`BSplCLib::NbPoles`, `Epsilon`, `MaxDegree`). The sketch's knot vector maps to them by runs of equal values, and a periodic spline's `poles + 1` values become knots of multiplicity 1, in OCCT's periodic convention, which the sketch's evaluation shares (kernel.md section 2.5 items 1 and 5, open issue 24).
5. **The shim measures, the facade decides.** Before it chains a loop, the facade gives every `Bspline` to the shim's `measure_splines`, which returns its points at the ends of its range, its length and its signed area term, and the revolve gives each to `spline_extremes`. Every check, message and orientation stays in Rust, in kernel.md section 3.4.2's order. Conics are measured in Rust in closed form, with their nearest points found by Newton's method.
6. **Checks with the facade's own messages.** Sizes of conics, the spline structure that OCCT requires, the range, closed curves alone in their loop, zero length and a conic arc's ends on its conic, each with its `InvalidInput` sentence of kernel.md section 4.2. Rules that OCCT imposes beyond sketch.md section 3.20.5 go to track 1A (open issue 24).
7. **Exact extremes for the revolve.** The half-plane check takes each conic's extremes in closed form and each spline's within 1e-9 mm by Bezier segments, the convex hull of their poles and subdivision. The poles alone never decide.

## Alternatives considered

Every option is open source, built on OCCT and on Partsmith's own code. The first criterion of the rule picks none of them. The rule then takes the safest, then the industry standard, then this work's recommendation. Each choice is Accepted (owner's rule of 2026-11-05).

1. *A piece of a spline or conic* (decided by the decisions of 2026-11-19, item 2). Options: (a) the whole curve plus a parameter range, trimmed in the kernel; (b) the sketch inserts knots until the piece is a B-spline of its own, whose ends are its vertices; (c) the end points only, the kernel projecting them to parameters. (a) is the safest: it is exact, unambiguous on closed and self-crossing curves, and lets the sketch's region union merge pieces as unions of ranges. (b) moves the sketch's knot insertion into the kernel's input and rounds the piece's poles. (c) is ambiguous where a point lies at several parameters. A trimmed basis curve is how OCCT itself describes a piece (`Geom2d_TrimmedCurve`), the industry standard.
2. *Where splines are evaluated for the checks and the orientation.* Options: (i) the shim measures and the facade decides; (ii) a second spline evaluation in this crate's Rust; (iii) the shim checks and orients every loop. The rule takes (i), the safest: OCCT evaluates the curve the shim builds, so what is checked is what is built, and every message and its order stays in Rust, where M1 put them. (ii) keeps a second evaluator in step with the sketch's and OCCT's. (iii) moves M1's tested orientation code into C++ and spreads the checks over two languages.
3. *Conic arcs by points or by parameters.* Options: (i) `start` and `end` points, the parameters taken from the nearest points; (ii) a parameter range as for splines. The rule takes (i): it is the form `Arc` already has, the points are the sketch's shared vertices, and a conic's nearest point is unique within `LINEAR` of it, so (ii) would only carry the same information twice.
4. *Extremes of a spline for the revolve.* Options: (i) exact within 1e-9 mm by subdivision; (ii) the convex hull of the poles. The rule takes (i), the safest: (ii) refuses profiles whose poles cross the axis while the curve does not, so the kernel would refuse what the features accept and its hint would call the failure a bug.
5. *An ellipse whose minor radius is the larger.* Options: (i) swap its axes in the kernel; (ii) refuse it. The rule takes (i): sketch.md section 3.20.1 allows it, and the swapped ellipse is the same curve.

## Consequences

- kernel.md sections 2.1, 2.5, 3.4.2 item 7, 3.4.4, 3.10 item 9, 4.2 and tests 191 to 210 state it. The bridge's `ProfileCurveData` gains fields and `make_profile` a slice of `SplineData` (kernel.md section 2.5 item 7).
- partsmith-features converts each new `ProfileCurve` variant to its `PlanarCurve` one to one (features.md section 3.7, track 1C), and passes a split spline's piece unchanged.
- sketch.md section 2.7's `ProfileCurve` (track 1A, 2026-11-19) carries this geometry: its `Bspline` has `range: Option<[f64; 2]>` and a sketch knot vector that features.md section 3.7 groups into knots and multiplicities. sketch.md sections 3.20.5 and 3.20.8 should refuse the splines OCCT refuses (open issue 24), which goes to track 1A.
- The edges report `Ellipse`, `Parabola`, `Hyperbola` and `BsplineCurve` (kernel.md section 3.6 item 3), and their extruded and revolved faces `Extrusion` and `Revolution`, which naming's fingerprints already hold.
- The core size of the change is recorded (kernel.md section 5, [ADR 0076](0076-occt-built-for-the-web-at-oz.md) decision 6).
