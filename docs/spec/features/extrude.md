# Feature: extrude

Status: M1 spec, with M2 item 17's extents (since M2, [ADR W2-1C-1](../../adr/W2-1C-1-extent-records-of-extrude-and-revolve.md) to [ADR W2-1C-5](../../adr/W2-1C-5-names-of-the-new-faces-and-the-order-of-the-through-all-checks.md)). Type name `extrude`. Shared contracts: [features.md](../features.md). Naming rule: [naming.md](../naming.md) section 3.6.2. Kernel operation: [kernel.md](../kernel.md) sections 2.6 and 3.4.3.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Extrude sweeps closed regions of one sketch along the sketch plane normal and combines the resulting solid with a body.

### In M1

- Profile: one or more closed regions of one sketch (regions with holes allowed); several regions are swept as their union.
- Extents: `distance` (one side), `symmetric` (centered on the sketch plane), `two_sides` (independent distances on each side), `through_all` (through the whole target body in one direction).
- `reverse` to extrude against the sketch normal.
- Ops `new`, `join`, `cut`, `intersect` with the shared body semantics.

### In M2

The M2 plan's item 17, on the constructions kernel.md section 8.5 measured:

- Extents `up_to_face` (a planar or cylindrical face of any body), `up_to_body` (where the extrude first meets a body) and `up_to_vertex` (the plane through a vertex of a body, normal to the direction), each refused with a message of its own when the target lies behind the start or on its plane, beside the extrude's path, or only partly in it ([ADR W2-1C-2](../../adr/W2-1C-2-up-to-targets-direction-and-reach-checks.md), [ADR W2-1C-4](../../adr/W2-1C-4-up-to-a-vertex-and-the-vertex-selection-kind.md)).
- `through_all_both`: through the whole body on both sides of the sketch plane.
- A taper angle on `distance`, `through_all` and `up_to_vertex`, and on `up_to_face` with a flat face ([ADR W2-1C-3](../../adr/W2-1C-3-taper.md)). The key exists on `up_to_body` too, for track 4A, and a taper up to a body or a cylindrical face is refused until spike 8.10 measures it.
- A start at an offset from the sketch plane, or on the plane of a flat face parallel to it ([ADR W2-1C-1](../../adr/W2-1C-1-extent-records-of-extrude-and-revolve.md)).
- The selection kind `vertex` for the up-to vertex (model.md section 2.12).
- Through all with Operation New body reports a sweep that points away before the body rule (open issue 6, [ADR W2-1C-5](../../adr/W2-1C-5-names-of-the-new-faces-and-the-order-of-the-through-all-checks.md)).
- Profile curves of item 14 (ellipses, elliptical arcs, parabolas, hyperbolas, conics and splines) in every extent that sweeps a plain prism: `distance`, `symmetric`, `two_sides`, `through_all`, `through_all_both` and `up_to_vertex`, without a taper (features.md section 3.7).

### Later

- Up to a face of another surface kind (cones among them), a taper up to a body or a cylindrical face, and up to a face or a body or a taper on a profile of several separate pieces or with a conic or spline curve: track 4A widens these in M2 from spike 8.10's recorded results (kernel.md section 8.10), from test 126 on (open issue 7).
- Thin-wall extrusion of open or closed profiles: M2 item 29 (wave 3).
- Two-sided and symmetric tapers, a start on a face that is not flat and parallel to the sketch, up to a sketch point or a datum point, and two sides with a different extent on each: M3.
- Extrusion along a direction other than the sketch normal: M3.
- Extruding body faces instead of sketch regions: M3.
- Profiles that mix regions of several sketches: M3.
- Surface extrusion of open profiles: M4.

The milestones of the Later list are routed in m2-plan.md section 5 by the stage 1 integrator's dated note.

## 2. Public API

```rust
/// Extrudes closed sketch regions along the sketch normal and combines the result with a body.
pub struct Extrude;

impl Feature for Extrude {
    const TYPE: &'static str = "extrude";
    type Params = ExtrudeParams;
}

/// Params of an extrude feature.
pub struct ExtrudeParams {
    /// Target body.
    pub body: Id,
    /// Closed regions of one sketch.
    pub profile: ProfileRef,
    /// Where the extrusion starts. Default `SketchPlane`, omitted when it is the default. Since M2.
    pub start: ExtrudeStart,
    /// How far the extrusion goes.
    pub extent: ExtrudeExtent,
    /// Extrude against the sketch normal. Default false, omitted when false.
    pub reverse: bool,
    /// How the extrusion combines with the body. Required.
    pub op: BodyOp,
}

/// Where an extrusion starts, measured along the sketch normal whatever `reverse` says. Since M2.
#[serde(tag = "kind", rename_all = "snake_case", deny_unknown_fields)]
pub enum ExtrudeStart {
    /// On the sketch plane.
    SketchPlane,
    /// On the plane `expr` from the sketch plane along its normal. Length, any sign.
    Offset { expr: Expr },
    /// On the plane of a flat face of `body` parallel to the sketch plane.
    Face { body: Id, face: Reference },
}

/// How far an extrusion goes, measured along its direction from its start.
#[serde(tag = "kind", rename_all = "snake_case", deny_unknown_fields)]
pub enum ExtrudeExtent {
    /// From the start to `expr` along the direction. Length, greater than 0.
    Distance { expr: Expr, taper: Option<Expr> },
    /// Total length `expr`, centered on the start. Length, greater than 0.
    Symmetric { expr: Expr },
    /// `expr` along the direction and `opposite_expr` against it. Lengths, at least 0, not both 0.
    TwoSides { expr: Expr, opposite_expr: Expr },
    /// From the start through the whole target body along the direction.
    ThroughAll { taper: Option<Expr> },
    /// Through the whole target body on both sides of the sketch plane. Since M2.
    ThroughAllBoth,
    /// From the start up to a planar or cylindrical face of `body`. Since M2.
    UpToFace { body: Id, face: Reference, taper: Option<Expr> },
    /// From the start up to where the extrusion first meets body `target`. Since M2.
    UpToBody { target: Id, taper: Option<Expr> },
    /// From the start up to the plane through a vertex of `body`, normal to the direction. Since M2.
    UpToVertex { body: Id, vertex: Reference, taper: Option<Expr> },
}
```

`taper` (since M2) is an angle expression, omitted when absent: the angle each lateral face turns away from the direction, outward for a positive angle ([ADR W2-1C-3](../../adr/W2-1C-3-taper.md)). Only the variants above that list it have the key: a `taper` in a `symmetric`, `two_sides` or `through_all_both` extent fails as invalid params, as any unknown key inside the extent does.

JSON (canonical key order):

```json
{"id": "f-ext1", "type": "extrude", "body": "b-1",
 "profile": {"sketch": "f-sk1", "regions": [{"key": "e-1,e-2,e-3,e-4", "fp": "c=40,25|a=4000"}]},
 "extent": {"kind": "distance", "expr": "30 mm"}, "op": "new"}
```

Other extents: `{"kind": "symmetric", "expr": "30 mm"}`, `{"kind": "two_sides", "expr": "20 mm", "opposite_expr": "10 mm"}`, `{"kind": "through_all"}`. With reverse: `"reverse": true` between `extent` and `op`.

Since M2: `{"kind": "through_all_both"}`, `{"kind": "up_to_face", "body": "b-2", "face": {"q": "f-box2/bottom", "fp": "plane|n=0,0,-1|c=40,25,40|a=10000|adj=4,4"}}`, `{"kind": "up_to_body", "target": "b-2"}`, `{"kind": "up_to_vertex", "body": "b-2", "vertex": {"q": "vertex(f-box2/bottom|f-box2/front|f-box2/left)", "fp": "point|c=-10,-25,40|adj=3,3"}}`, and a taper last in the extent: `{"kind": "distance", "expr": "30 mm", "taper": "5 deg"}`. A start sits between `profile` and `extent`: `"start": {"kind": "offset", "expr": "10 mm"}` or `"start": {"kind": "face", "body": "b-2", "face": {"q": "f-box2/top", "fp": "plane|n=0,0,1|c=40,25,50|a=10000|adj=4,4"}}`. A stored `"start": {"kind": "sketch_plane"}` canonicalizes to no `start` key.

### Python

Dedicated form: `extrude(profile, distance, regions=..., symmetric=..., opposite_distance=..., reverse=..., through_all=..., op=...)` (python-api.md section 2.7), and since M2 the keywords `through_all_both_ways=`, `end_face=`, `end_body=`, `end_vertex=`, `taper_angle=`, `start_offset=` and `start_face=` after `through_all`, in that order, each named after its field's label ([ADR 0048](../../adr/0048-python-keywords-gui-round-trip-and-python-budgets.md) decision 1, [ADR W2-1C-1](../../adr/W2-1C-1-extent-records-of-extrude-and-revolve.md) decision 7). The hook produces it with the shared rules of features.md section 3.11:

- `profile`: the sketch's variable, plus `regions=[Region("<key>", "<fp>"), ...]` when the stored regions are not all of the sketch's regions in solved order.
- `distance` extent: `distance` is `extent.expr`. `symmetric`: the same plus `symmetric=True`. `two_sides`: the same plus `opposite_distance=` with `extent.opposite_expr` ([ADR 0048](../../adr/0048-python-keywords-gui-round-trip-and-python-budgets.md)). `through_all`: no `distance`, and `through_all=True`.
- Since M2: `through_all_both`: no `distance`, and `through_all_both_ways=True`. `up_to_face`: no `distance`, and `end_face=` the face as `PythonValue::plane` writes a face, `Ref("<q>", "<fp>", part=<part>)` with the variable of the extent's `body`. `up_to_body`: no `distance`, and `end_body=` the variable of `target`. `up_to_vertex`: no `distance`, and `end_vertex=` the vertex as `end_face` writes a face. `taper_angle=` with `extent.taper` when the extent has one. `start_offset=` with `start.expr` for an `offset` start, `start_face=` as `end_face` for a `face` start, and neither for `sketch_plane`.
- `reverse=True` when `reverse` is true; `op` when it differs from the default op of `body`.
- Every extent has a dedicated form; the hook returns `None` only when a `PythonNames` lookup it needs does (the profile's sketch has no variable, or no solved regions).

With `f-sk1` as `sketch_1` and op `new` as the default, the JSON above is `extrude(sketch_1, 30*mm, id="f-ext1")`, and with the two-sided extent above it is `extrude(sketch_1, 20*mm, opposite_distance=10*mm, id="f-ext1")`. With `b-2` as `part_2`, the up-to-face extent above is `extrude(sketch_1, end_face=Ref("f-box2/bottom", "plane|n=0,0,-1|c=40,25,40|a=10000|adj=4,4", part=part_2), id="f-ext1")`, and the tapered distance with the offset start above is `extrude(sketch_1, 30*mm, taper_angle=5*deg, start_offset=10*mm, id="f-ext1")`. Generic form (features.md section 3.11):

```python
feature(
    "extrude",
    id="f-ext1",
    body="b-1",
    profile={"sketch": "f-sk1", "regions": [{"key": "e-1,e-2,e-3,e-4", "fp": "c=40,25|a=4000"}]},
    extent={"kind": "distance", "expr": "30 mm"},
    op="new",
)
```

## 3. Behavior

### 3.1 Validation

- `profile.regions` is not empty, else `EmptySelection { field: "profile", noun: region }`.
- Since M2: a `through_all_both` extent has the `sketch_plane` start, else `StartNotAllowed` (field `start.kind`).

### 3.2 Dependencies

- `features`: `[profile.sketch]`.
- `bodies_written`: `[body]`. `bodies_read` (since M2): the `body` of a `face` start, then the `body` of an `up_to_face` or `up_to_vertex` extent or the `target` of an `up_to_body` one, in that order, each unless it is `body`, without duplicates. In M1 it is empty.
- `expressions`, in params order: `start.expr` for an `offset` start (Length); then `distance` and `symmetric`: `("extent.expr", expr, Length)`; `two_sides`: `("extent.expr", expr, Length)`, `("extent.opposite_expr", opposite_expr, Length)`; `through_all`, `through_all_both` and the up-to kinds: none; then `("extent.taper", taper, Angle)` when the extent has a taper.

### 3.3 Build

The checks run in the order of the steps, and the first that fails is reported ([ADR W2-1C-2](../../adr/W2-1C-2-up-to-targets-direction-and-reach-checks.md) decision 6). N is the sketch placement normal and o its origin.

1. Resolve the profile with `ctx.profile("profile", ...)` (features.md section 3.7). Region warnings and notices go into the output, and the match's successions (`ResolvedProfile::successions`) go to naming in step 8.
2. Direction d: N; its negation when `reverse` is true and the extent is neither `symmetric` nor `through_all_both`. Those two ignore `reverse` (the solid and its names are the same either way).
3. **Start** (since M2). The start plane lies at s along N from the sketch plane: 0 for `sketch_plane`; `start.expr` for `offset`, at most the modeling limit in size (`OutOfRange` `AtMost(MODEL_LIMIT)` above it and `AtLeast(-MODEL_LIMIT)` below its negation, features.md section 3.4); for `face`, `ctx.resolve("start.face", start.body, face, Face)` with its warnings and notices into the output, and every resolved face must be planar (`StartFace` with `why: NotFlat` on the first that is not), all of them in one plane (`NotCoplanar`, field `start.face`), and that plane parallel to the sketch plane, its normal within 1e-9 rad of N or of -N (`StartFace` with `why: NotParallel`); s is then the signed distance (p - o) · N of a point p of that plane. Along d, the start plane lies at t = s (N · d). With the `sketch_plane` start, t is 0 and the steps below are M1's.
4. **Interval** [lo, hi] along d, measured from the sketch plane, with the positive length rule of features.md section 3.4:
   - `distance`: L = `extent.expr`; L > 0 (at least `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`) else `OutOfRange` (`GreaterThan(0)`, or `AtLeast(LINEAR_TOLERANCE)` for an L above 0, features.md section 3.4); then L at most the modeling limit, else `OutOfRange` (`AtMost(MODEL_LIMIT)`, features.md section 3.4), as for every length below; interval [t, t + L].
   - `symmetric`: L > 0 likewise; interval [t - L/2, t + L/2].
   - `two_sides`: a = `extent.expr` ≥ 0 and b = `extent.opposite_expr` ≥ 0, else `OutOfRange` (`AtLeast(0)`) on that field; a + b > 0 (at least `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`) else `BothSidesZero`; interval [t - b, t + a].
   - `through_all`: op `new` on an empty body gives `ThroughAllNeedsBody { body }`. With another op, an empty body gives `BodyEmpty { field: body, body, use_new: true }`. Otherwise let D be the largest signed distance (p - o) · d over the eight corners p of the body's tight bounding box. D ≤ t gives `ThroughAllMisses { body, reverse_hits }`, where `reverse_hits` is true when the same test along -d from the same start plane (start -t, D' the largest (p - o) · (-d)) gives D' > -t and the tool swept along -d over [-t, D' + 1 mm] shares more than `VOLUME_TOLERANCE` times the body's volume with it (one kernel `common`, on this error path only); only then is the fix Turn on Reverse offered. Then op `new` gives `BodyNotEmpty { body, target: None, new_body: false }`: the body holds a solid, which Operation New body cannot use, and a new empty body would have nothing to go through. Only Join is offered. Since M2 this test comes after `ThroughAllMisses`, and the Join it offers builds (open issue 6). Interval [t, D + 1 mm].
   - `through_all_both` (since M2): op `new` gives `ThroughAllNeedsBody { body }` on an empty body and `BodyNotEmpty { body, target: None, new_body: false }` on a body with a solid; another op on an empty body gives `BodyEmpty { field: body, body, use_new: true }`. With D the largest (p - o) · d and D' the largest (p - o) · (-d) over the eight corners, the interval is [-(max(D', 0) + 1 mm), max(D, 0) + 1 mm]: the whole body on both sides of the sketch plane, and the sketch plane itself. It never misses along d; a body beside the profile gives `apply_op`'s `NoEffect` in step 8.
   - `up_to_vertex` (since M2): `ctx.resolve("extent.vertex", extent.body, vertex, Vertex)`, with its warnings and notices into the output. Each resolved vertex at position v gives the end e = (v - o) · d. Every resolved vertex must give the first one's e within `LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, else `UpToAmbiguous { field: extent.vertex, body: extent.body, reference: vertex, elements, kind: Vertex }` with the resolved vertices, which the error offers as candidates. Then, with e the first vertex's: when e - t ≥ `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` the interval is [t, e]; when e - t ≤ -`LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, `UpToMisses` with `how: Behind { reverse_hits: true }` (reversed, the vertex lies ahead, and Turn on Reverse always builds); otherwise `UpToMisses` with `how: OnStart`.
   - `up_to_face` and `up_to_body` (since M2): lo is t, and the end comes from the split in step 7.
5. **Taper** (since M2), for an extent with `taper`: θ = `extent.taper` in radians; θ > -π/2 else `OutOfRange` (`GreaterThan(-90 deg)`), θ < π/2 else `OutOfRange` (`LessThan(90 deg)`), on `extent.taper`. A θ within 1e-9 rad of 0 is no taper: the build is the one without the key, with the same solid and names.
6. **Profile.** Build the union of the resolved regions as kernel faces (`ResolvedProfile::faces`, features.md section 3.7) as in M1: the boundary pieces two selected regions share are dropped, consecutive pieces of one entity that continue the same line or circle are merged, and `make_profile` gets the curves tagged by sketch entity (sketch.md section 3.19). Since M2, for `up_to_face` and `up_to_body`, a union of more than one face gives `UpToProfile { limit: Parts { count } }`, and a curve converted from an `ellipse`, `elliptical_arc`, `parabola`, `hyperbola`, `conic`, `bspline`, `fit_spline` or `fixed_spline` entity gives `UpToProfile { limit: Curve { sketch, entity } }` with the first such entity in curve order. Then, for a taper (θ not 0), the same tests give `TaperProfile { field: extent.taper, limit }`. Holes in the one face are allowed in both.
7. **Target and sweep.** Sweep the profile faces with the kernel (kernel.md section 2.6; the extent, taper and revolve-angle calls of track 1B's ADR W2-1B-2):
   - the plain extents and `up_to_vertex`: `extrude(profile, d, lo, hi)`, or with a taper the drafted prism over the same interval with its neutral plane on the start plane;
   - `up_to_face`: `ctx.resolve("extent.face", extent.body, face, Face)`, with its warnings and notices into the output. When it resolves to several faces, its warnings and notices offer no `KeepMatch`: the field holds one reference, and `KeepMatch` would store the first face alone (engine.md section 3.22), which would change where the extrude may end. Every resolved face must be planar or cylindrical, else `UpToSurface { field: extent.face, element, surface }` with the stored reference and the geometry kind of the first face that is not (naming.md section 2.1). Several resolved faces must lie on one plane (the coplanar test of features.md section 3.6) or on one cylinder (the same axis within 1e-9 rad and 1e-6 mm, the same radius within 1e-6 mm), else `UpToAmbiguous { field: extent.face, body: extent.body, reference: face, elements, kind: Face }` with the resolved faces, which the error offers as candidates. Planar faces whose plane is parallel to the start plane (its normal within 1e-9 rad of N or of -N) and within `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` of it (|(p - o) · N - s| ≤ `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` for a point p of that plane) give `UpToMisses { field: extent.face, body: extent.body, element: Some(face), how: OnStart }`, before the kernel runs: the extrude would have no length, as up to a vertex on the start plane (a sketch on a face, extruded up to that face). The kernel splits the prism, plain or drafted, from t along d at the faces' surface (kernel.md section 8.5). It gets every resolved face (`ExtrudeEnd::Face`, kernel.md section 3.4.3), and an end sample lies on the target when one of them holds it. A face that an earlier feature split into pieces is reached across all of them;
   - `up_to_body`: the `target` body must hold a solid, else `BodyEmpty { field: extent.target, body: target, use_new: false }`. The kernel splits the prism from t along d at the target's solid.
   - With a taper, the end must be a flat face: `up_to_body` gives `TaperTarget { field: extent.taper, target: Body }` and `up_to_face` on cylindrical faces `TaperTarget { field: extent.taper, target: Cylinder }`, before the kernel runs (the kernel drafts only over an offset or up to a planar face, track 1B's ADR W2-1B-2 decision 2).

   The kernel tells how the prism met the target by the kind of its error, never by a sentence (ADR W2-1C-2 decision 4; the kinds of track 1B's ADR W2-1B-2 decision 3, kernel.md section 3.4.3 items 3 and 5). No error means reached: every end face lies on the target's resolved faces (up to a face), and the kept piece holds no part of the prism's far cap. `TargetPartlyReached` (some end samples outside the faces, or the far cap kept) gives `UpToPartly { field, body, element }`. `TargetStartsInside`, up to a body only, comes before the kernel builds any prism, when a sample of the profile moved to the start plane lies inside a solid of the target, and gives `UpToStartsInside { field: extent.target, body: target }`. `TargetNotReached` (no image of the target in the kept piece, every end sample outside the faces, or a through length below `LINEAR`) makes the feature run the same split along -d from the start plane, at -t along -d, on this error path only: no error gives `UpToMisses` with `how: Behind { reverse_hits: true }`, `TargetPartlyReached` or `TargetStartsInside` gives `Behind { reverse_hits: false }`, and `TargetNotReached` gives `how: Beside`. A profile that lies on the boundary of the body it runs up to, such as a sketch on a face of that body, was not measured (kernel.md section 3.4.3 item 3): until spike 8.10's Results it gets the outcome the kernel's checks give, mapped as above, and no test of stage 1 pins it (open issue 7).

   With a taper (θ not 0), an error of kind `Failed`, `InvalidResult` or `EmptyResult`, from either split, gives `TaperTooSteep { field: extent.taper, angle: θ, cause }` ([ADR W2-1C-3](../../adr/W2-1C-3-taper.md) decision 6). The kernel does not tell a failure of the draft from a failure of the split: up to a flat face, a failed split reads as a closing taper too. This mapping is provisional until spike 8.10's Results (open issue 7). `Canceled` gives `Canceled` (features.md section 2.6), and every other kind, and every failure without a taper, gives `Kernel`.
8. Name the tool's faces (section 3.4), with the profile sketch's motion as the new faces' motion (`SketchResult::motion`, features.md section 3.9), and call `apply_op(ctx, body, op, vec![tool], Some(Direction { direction: d, sketch }))`. `sketch` is `Some((&profile.sketch, &placement))`, with `placement` the profile sketch's `SketchResult::placement`, when the extrude starts on its sketch: the `sketch_plane` start, or an `offset` start whose s is within `LINEAR_TOLERANCE` of 0. For any other start it is `None`, and `apply_op` then offers no `Attach` (features.md section 3.15): the start plane would move with the sketch, and the attached extrude would not start on the face the fix names. When the result changes nothing or leaves a separate piece, `apply_op` reports why (features.md section 3.15: `NoEffect` with `Inside`, `Away`, `Short`, `Beside` or `Covers`, `SeparateSolid`), and the extrude answers `reverse_hits(ctx, body, tools)` by sweeping the same profile along -d over [lo - 2t, hi - 2t]: the build's interval mirrored through the start plane, which is M1's same interval [lo, hi] when t is 0. Turn on Reverse is offered only when that sweep reaches the body. For an `Inside` join with `reverse` true and an extent that is not `symmetric` or `through_all_both`, the same sweep along -d decides whether it adds to the body, and Turn off Reverse is offered only then (features.md section 3.15). The up-to kinds leave `reverse_hits` false and offer neither: reversed, the face, body or vertex they run up to lies behind the start, and the next build would fail with `up_to_misses` (`Behind`), whose own fix turns Reverse back (test 75). A reversed extrude gets no `Attach`, since its sweep runs against the sketch's normal.
9. **Ends outside the body.** With op `cut`, an `end-cap` (or `end-cap#n`) of the tool that is not in the result records an `AbsentFace` in `FeatureOutput::absent` (features.md section 2.7): `why: Through` when no `start-cap` (or `start-cap#n`) of the tool is in the result either, so the cut goes all the way through and a reference to its floor is explained as `is no longer made by 'Extrude 2', which goes all the way through the body` and never moved to a rim (corpus R12); otherwise `why: EndOutside`, explained as `is no longer made by 'Extrude 2', which now ends outside the body` (a cut that starts inside the body, corpus R30 (a)). Since M2, a `through_all_both` cut with neither cap in the result also records each `start-cap` absent with `why: Through`: both of its caps are ends.

### 3.4 Element names

| Name | Face |
| --- | --- |
| `<id>/start-cap` | Cap at lo (on the start plane for a one-sided extent; on the side opposite d for symmetric, two sides and through all both ways). |
| `<id>/end-cap` | Cap at hi. Up to a face, the image of the cutting face on the target's surface; up to a body, each face of the body the extrude ends on, which the kernel gives the role `Target` (since M2). |
| `<id>/lateral/<entity id>` | Side face swept from the boundary piece that sketch entity `<entity id>` produced, tapered or not. |

Successions of the profile match are passed to `NamingMap::build` as `added_successions` of the lateral faces, kept apart from merge aliases (naming.md section 3.6.2, features.md section 3.9): when a line of the sketch is deleted and drawn again in its place, extended by a collinear line, or replaced by several lines, the map records that the old entity's face continues in the faces made from the new entities (`NamingMap::successions`), so references to it follow with a notice or a warning (features.md section 3.8, tests 47 to 50).

Several separate caps (disjoint regions) are numbered `start-cap#n` and `end-cap#n`; an entity that still contributes several separate boundary pieces after the union gives `lateral/<entity id>#n`, both in naming.md's geometric order. Boundaries shared by two selected regions produce no face, and the pieces of one entity on either side of a dropped boundary give one face, so a union of regions has the faces of the single region covering the same area. Faces of the body keep their names; caps or sides that end up inside the body after a join or cut disappear.

Since M2 ([ADR W2-1C-5](../../adr/W2-1C-5-names-of-the-new-faces-and-the-order-of-the-through-all-checks.md)): an end up to a body that meets several faces of the body gives one end cap per face, numbered `end-cap#n` in naming.md's geometric order. A tapered lateral face keeps the name of the untapered one. An end cap that a join or cut makes one face with a face of the body takes part in naming.md's merge rule unchanged (section 3.3 step 2): the merged face takes the smaller name and the other becomes its alias.

## 5. Performance

- Op `new` with a 4-curve profile: 3 ms. With a 50-curve profile: 15 ms.
- Join or cut against a 200-face body: 60 ms.
- Profile with 20 regions: 40 ms.
- Since M2, provisional until wave 4 sets each row from its measured median plus 20 percent ([ADR 0057](../../adr/0057-budgets-set-from-the-measured-median-plus-20-percent.md), m2-plan.md gate 10): `RECT` up to a face, op new, 30 ms (the target of kernel.md section 8.5, measured at 4.9 to 8.0 ms). A taper of 5 degrees on the 100-edge profile of kernel.md section 5 over 10 mm, op new, 60 ms (measured at 50.7 to 57.4 ms, with 4 of 20 set medians over 60 ms). Up to a vertex and through all both ways: the rows of a distance and of through all.
